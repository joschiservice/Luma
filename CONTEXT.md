# Exception Desk

Commerce exceptions are investigated, reviewed, executed and verified using retained evidence.

## Language

**Case observation**:
A confirmed view of a case, its proposed resolution and its execution progress at matching case and diagnosis revisions. Audit history and evidence history describe retained events and may be unavailable independently of this view.
_Avoid_: Case verification, resolution confirmation

**Resolution run**:
A retained record of the resolution selected for an incident and diagnosis revision, with the evidence and provenance available at selection time.
_Avoid_: Execution attempt

**Learning event**:
An append-only operator observation about a resolution run, such as a cause assessment, repair, intervention, outcome or recurrence. A claimed verified outcome must agree with the incident’s authoritative verification.
_Avoid_: Execution authorization
