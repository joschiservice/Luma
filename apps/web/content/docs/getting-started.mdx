Luma works across the systems your business already uses, such as Shopify and JTL-Wawi. It notices when work stops moving, finds out why, makes the changes your team has agreed on and checks that the fix worked. Your team spends its time on the decisions that need a person.

## Choose your data region

Before creating an account, choose the region approved for your business. A suggestion from your browser settings does not select it for you. Only available regions can accept new accounts. Your account and new workspaces belong to that region, and accounts in other regions are separate. Workspace creation shows the region again.

Luma stores and processes your workspace data in the selected region. Connected providers and services you authorize, such as email or AI services, may process data elsewhere. Review those services before connecting them.

## How Luma handles a problem

Take an order that’s paid in your shop but hasn’t reached your warehouse:

1. **Notice.** Luma reads data from your connected systems and opens an issue when the order stops moving.
2. **Find the cause.** Luma checks each system involved and shows its evidence: what each system reported and when Luma last read it.
3. **Choose a fix.** Luma proposes a change that fits your workspace’s approval rules, or tells you what a person needs to do.
4. **Make the change.** If the change needs approval, it waits in **Needs you** until someone with approval permission approves it under your [workspace rules](/docs/workspace-settings#approvals). Then Luma sends it to the system.
5. **Check it worked.** Luma reads the system again. A system accepting a change doesn’t prove the order is moving, so an issue counts as resolved only once a fresh read confirms the outcome.

Every step is recorded, so you can always see who decided what and what Luma did.

## The main areas

The sidebar lists the areas your role includes:

- **Home**: what needs your attention, what Luma is handling and whether your systems are sending data. See [Home](/docs/home).
- **Needs you**: approvals, manual steps and outcome checks waiting for a person. See [Needs you](/docs/needs-you).
- **Issues**: operational problems with their evidence, proposed fixes and outcomes. See [Issues](/docs/issues).
- **All records**, inside the collapsible **Records** section: one search across orders, fulfillments, stock, SKUs, products and issues. See [Records](/docs/records).
- **Activity**: what people and Luma did in this workspace. See [Activity](/docs/activity).
- **Insights**: operational metrics, order flow and verified outcomes. See [Insights](/docs/insights).
- **Records**: [Orders](/docs/orders), [Customers](/docs/customers), [Products](/docs/products), [Returns](/docs/returns), [Inventory](/docs/inventory), [Fulfillment](/docs/fulfillment) and [Procurement](/docs/procurement), each with data from every connected system.
- **Manage apps**, **Data sync** and **Settings**: connect your systems, follow imports and manage your workspace. See [Apps and connections](/docs/apps-and-connections), [Data sync](/docs/data-sync) and [Workspace settings](/docs/workspace-settings).

**Operations** keeps [Inbox](/docs/inbox) and [Workflows](/docs/workflows) directly accessible. Installed apps add pages under **Installed apps**.

## Where to start

1. **Connect your systems.** Open **Manage apps**, install the app for your shop or ERP and add a connection. You need **Manage connections**. See [Apps and connections](/docs/apps-and-connections).
2. **Wait for the first data.** **Data sync** shows what Luma has imported from each system. Home tells you while a system hasn’t sent data yet.
3. **Check Home.** It shows how many decisions need you and what Luma is working on.
4. **Respond in Needs you.** Approve, reject or complete the work waiting for a person.
5. **Investigate issues.** Open an issue to see its cause, evidence and proposed fix.

If you set up the workspace, also [invite your team](/docs/workspace-settings#team) and decide on [approval rules](/docs/workspace-settings#approvals).

## Get help while you work

On pages with a guide, select **Help for this page** in the top bar. The guide opens beside your work and doesn’t change your filters or selection. To find your way around faster, see [Find your way around](/docs/navigation). To ask a question about your data, use [Ask Luma](/docs/ask-luma).

## Check that your records are linked

Open **Data sync → Automatic linking** after the imports complete. With one Shopify store, Luma links unique SKU codes and explicit order references from JTL-Wawi, Cin7 Core and Starshipit automatically. Review skipped conflicts and link the matching stock locations in **Inventory**. Linked SKUs and locations with current reads can be compared for stock issues. If you have multiple Shopify stores, preview and enable rules for the correct connection pairs.
