---
title: Decide what needs you
description: Find every approval, manual step and outcome check waiting on a person, understand what your response does, and respond once.
---

**Needs you** collects the decisions Luma can't make on its own, from issues, workflows and apps, in one queue. You don't need to know which workflow or app raised a decision to find it. Press G then N to open it. The sidebar shows how many decisions are waiting.

## What appears here

- **Approvals**: a proposed change that a second owner or admin must approve, such as an issue fix, a purchase-order draft from replenishment, sending a purchase order to a supplier, a Shopify stock correction or a fulfillment recovery. A supplier's changed quantity or date waits here too, to be accepted or followed up.
- **Ready to run**: an approved Shopify change that only its requester can run before it expires.
- **Outcome checks**: a change the source system accepted, or whose outcome is unknown, that no fresh read has confirmed yet. An accepted change is not a verified one.
- **Manual steps**: work Luma can't do itself, such as a correction in your ERP or chasing a supplier whose confirmation or delivery is overdue. See [Procurement](/docs/procurement).
- **Reviews**: evidence that is incomplete or conflicting, an issue someone escalated, or a fact Luma read from a supplier message that needs checking.

Decisions leave the queue as soon as they're answered, expire or no longer apply. An empty queue means nothing is waiting on a person. It doesn't mean every connection is healthy; Home shows connection health.

## How the queue is ordered

1. Proposals that expire within 30 minutes. Approval doesn't extend a proposal's deadline.
2. High-impact decisions.
3. Everything else, earliest deadline first, then oldest request.

Unknown impact is labeled **Impact not assessed** instead of being ranked as low.

Filter by area (orders, fulfillment, inventory, procurement) or by reason. **Group by cause** puts decisions with the same cause together, such as all stock corrections awaiting approval. Each still needs its own response; nothing is approved in bulk. Every filter and the selected decision are part of the address, so you can share a link or pin the view.

## Respond to a decision

Select a decision to see:

- **Why you're needed** and, if you can't respond yourself, who can.
- **Proposed change**: the exact source, record, location and quantities.
- **What your response does** for each available response.
- **Evidence and ownership**: when the evidence was observed, whether it's still current, the deadline, who requested it and who's looking after it.

**Review and respond** shows the same controls as the issue, workflow run or record the decision belongs to. Responding here is the one decision; the issue, run and record update without a second one. Before anything changes, the owner checks again that the proposal, its revision and its expiry are unchanged. If someone else already answered, or the proposal changed or expired, your response is refused and the current state is shown.

The person who requested a change can't approve it. Members can follow every decision; owners and admins respond to approvals.

Use J and K to move through the list, and Esc to close a decision.

## Assign decisions

Take a decision to show your team you're looking after it. Owners and admins can also hand a decision to another member. **Assigned to me** and **Unassigned** list the matching decisions. Assigning a decision doesn't change who may approve it.

## When a decision is missing

If Luma can't read one area's decisions, the page names it. Those decisions are missing from the list, not resolved; refresh to try again. The queue updates live and checks again every 30 seconds while live updates are interrupted.
