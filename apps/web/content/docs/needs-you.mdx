---
title: Decide what needs you
description: Find every approval, manual step and outcome check waiting for a person, take ownership, and respond once.
---

**Needs you** collects the decisions Luma can’t make on its own, from issues, Workflows, Procurement and apps, in one queue. You don’t need to know which Workflow or app raised a decision to find it. Open it from the sidebar, or press **G** then **N**. The sidebar shows how many decisions are waiting.

## What appears here

- **Approvals**: a proposed change that needs approval under your [workspace rules](/docs/workspace-settings#approvals), such as an issue fix, a purchase order drafted by replenishment, sending a purchase order, a Shopify stock correction or a fulfillment recovery. A supplier’s changed quantity or date waits here too, to be accepted or followed up.
- **Ready to run**: an approved Shopify change that only its requester can send, before it expires.
- **Outcome checks**: a change the connected system accepted, or whose outcome is unclear, that no separate check has confirmed yet. Accepted isn’t the same as verified.
- **Manual steps**: work Luma can’t do itself, such as a correction in your ERP, chasing a supplier whose confirmation or delivery is overdue, or a follow-up a [Workflow](/docs/workflows) handed to a person, like a return still waiting for its refund.
- **Unclear evidence**: incomplete or conflicting evidence, an issue someone escalated, or a fact Luma read from a supplier message that needs checking.

Decisions leave the queue as soon as they’re answered, expire or no longer apply. An empty queue means no decision is waiting for a person. It doesn’t mean every connection is healthy; Home shows connection health.

## Choose a view

- **All**: every decision you can see.
- **Assigned to me**: decisions you took or someone assigned to you.
- **Unassigned**: decisions nobody is looking after yet.

The tabs also include the views you and your team saved. Opening **Needs you** from the sidebar returns to the view you used last. The counts on the tabs, and in the sidebar, always cover the whole queue you can see, whatever you filter.

## Find decisions

Search by title. Your search text stays on the page: it isn’t saved in the page address or in views, and it’s cleared when you leave or reload the page.

**Owner**, **Area** (Orders, Fulfillment, Inventory, Procurement or Other operations) and **Reason** above the queue narrow it in one click. **Owner** finds decisions you hold, decisions nobody holds or the decisions of one person; a view saved with **Me** shows each person their own. For anything else, choose **Filter**: **Impact**, **Deadline** (a date range, the next few days, or decisions without one) and **Waiting since**. **Clear** removes every filter and the search.

The sort menu after **Display** names the current order. Choose it to sort by deadline or by how long decisions have waited, or to flip the direction. Decisions without a deadline come last when you sort by deadline.

Your view, filters and sort are part of the address, together with the selected decision, so you can share a link or pin the view. Links from before views, such as one area from Insights, open the same filters.

## Arrange the queue

Select **Display** to choose what each decision shows, such as its type, owner, impact, deadline or waiting time, to group the queue, and to switch to **Compact** density. Every row keeps its status, so nothing waiting on you is hidden.

**Group by** puts decisions together by area, reason, owner or cause, such as all stock corrections waiting for approval. Each group shows how many decisions it has; **Show more** loads more. A group marked incomplete may be missing decisions from an area that didn’t answer. Each decision still needs its own response; nothing is approved in bulk.

Changed filters, sorting, grouping or display mark the view **Modified**; select **Save**, **Save as new view** or **Reset**. Density and collapsed groups are your own settings.

## How the queue is ordered

1. Decisions that expire within 30 minutes. Approving doesn’t extend a deadline.
2. **High impact** decisions.
3. Everything else, earliest deadline first, then oldest request.

Impact Luma hasn’t assessed is labeled **Impact not assessed** instead of being ranked as low.

## What each status means

| Status                           | What it means                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Needs your approval**          | You can approve or reject this change.                                                               |
| **Ready for you to run**         | You requested this change and it’s approved. Send it before it expires.                              |
| **Check the outcome**            | The change was sent, but no check has confirmed its result.                                          |
| **Manual step required**         | Someone has to act outside Luma or on the record.                                                    |
| **Review evidence**              | The evidence is unclear, or someone escalated the issue.                                             |
| **Proposal expired**             | The approval window closed before anyone answered. Open the issue or record to review it again.      |
| **Waiting for another approver** | Your workspace requires another person to approve this change you requested. You can follow it here. |
| **Waiting for the requester**    | It’s approved, and only the person who requested it can run it.                                      |
| **Waiting for an approver**      | Your role can see this decision but can’t respond to it.                                             |
| **Outcome not confirmed**        | The result isn’t confirmed yet, and your role can’t act on it.                                       |

## Respond to a decision

Select a decision to see:

- **Why you’re needed** and, if you can’t respond yourself, why not.
- **Proposed change**: the exact system, record, location and quantities.
- **What your response does** for each response you can give.
- **Evidence and owner**: when the evidence was last read and whether it’s still current, the deadline, who requested it and who’s looking after it. **Outdated evidence** or **Evidence age unknown** tells you to check the record before you rely on it.

**Review and respond** shows the same controls as the issue, Workflow run or record the decision belongs to. Responding here is the decision; the issue, run or record updates without a second one. Before anything changes, Luma checks again that the proposal and its deadline are unchanged. If someone else already answered, or the proposal changed or expired, your response is refused and **This decision has left the queue** shows the current state.

Responding to an approval needs **Approve or reject changes**, and the person who requested a change can’t approve it. Some approvals need more: purchase orders also need **Manage procurement** and **Send purchase orders to JTL**, and Shopify changes need **Recover Shopify fulfillments** or **Correct Shopify inventory**.

## Take or assign a decision

Choose **Take it** to show your team you’re looking after a decision, or **Unassign me** to hand it back. People with **Assign decisions** can also **Take over** a decision someone else holds, or assign it to another member: choose **Assign to…** and type part of their name or email. Owning a decision doesn’t change who may approve it.

Members can take decisions and follow them, but their role doesn’t include **Approve or reject changes**. They see approvals as **Waiting for an approver**, with a note that their role can’t respond.

## Move through the queue

- **J** and **K**, or the arrow keys, move through the queue.
- **Space** opens the decision you're on beside the queue, or closes it. While it's open, **J** and **K** open the next and previous decision.
- **Enter** opens the decision you're on.
- **Esc** closes the decision and returns you to its row.
- **/** goes to search, **F** adds a filter and **?** lists these shortcuts.

Shortcuts don't work while you type in a field. When the queue refreshes, the decision you're on stays selected even if others move around it.

## When the queue is incomplete or offline

- **Some decisions couldn’t be loaded** names the area that didn’t answer. Its decisions are missing from the list, not resolved, even when the list is empty: **Decisions may be missing** then replaces the empty message. Choose **Refresh Needs you** to try again.
- **Needs you couldn’t be refreshed** means the list below may be out of date.
- **You’re offline**: the list shows the decisions Luma last loaded, and responses are paused until you reconnect.

The queue updates live. If live updates are interrupted, it still checks for changes every 30 seconds.
