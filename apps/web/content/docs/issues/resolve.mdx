---
title: Review a resolution
description: Check a proposed correction, record your decision, and confirm the outcome.
---

Open the resolution section of an issue to review the available next step. You will only see decisions that are permitted for you and the current issue.

## Read the proposed correction

Before approving, check:

1. **Target system and object:** confirm which connection and record will change.
2. **Proposed changes:** review the fields and values to be applied.
3. **Expected result:** understand what a successful correction should establish.
4. **Preconditions and evidence:** check what the proposal relies on.
5. **Policy and risk:** read the approval requirement and any restrictions.

If recovery is not specified, do not assume the change can be undone.

## Record a decision

Use the available controls to approve, reject, or escalate, and supply a reason when requested. A policy may require approval from another authorized operator. When automatic execution is allowed, no approval click is needed.

Luma checks current conditions for proposals that support a fresh review. If the proposed action changes, review the updated details before approving again. Outdated evidence, changed approval settings, or an unavailable capability can block a decision.

If there is no proposed correction, follow the investigation's recommended manual steps or escalate when that option is available.

## Handle an uncertain submission

If a connection problem interrupts a decision, its outcome may be uncertain. Use the receipt recovery controls shown on the page to check whether the decision was recorded. Follow the page's instructions before trying again.

## Confirm the outcome

After a decision, follow **Execution and verification**. These are separate from approval:

- **Execution** records whether the action ran.
- **Verification** checks the result using fresh evidence.
- **Resolution verified** confirms the correction with that evidence.
- **Closed by human disposition** records closure without proving a correction.

If progress is unavailable, refresh before relying on an old status. A successful submission or recorded approval alone does not confirm resolution.

Return to [Investigate an issue](/docs/issues/investigate) for guidance on findings and evidence, or [Find and prioritize issues](/docs/issues) to continue through your inbox.

## Inspect verified outcomes

On **Insights**, open **Verified outcomes & human work**. Choose an environment
and a detection window, then select a metric to inspect its incidents and evidence.
The same incident counts once across retries. A repeated problem appears as a new
linked incident. A later verification stays in the original detection cohort.

**Verified without help** requires known eligibility, independent verification
and complete attribution. Approvals, repairs and corrections count as human work.
Missing attribution stays unknown. Synthetic, partner-pilot and shadow cohorts
are separate from live results. Partial coverage describes recorded incidents;
it does not prove every provider record was observed.
