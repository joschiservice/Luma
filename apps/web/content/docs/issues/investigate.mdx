---
title: Investigate an issue
description: Read what Luma found, check how current the evidence is, and follow an issue from investigation to a confirmed result.
---

An issue keeps what Luma found, the data behind it, any change it proposes and everything that happened since. Read it before you decide what to do. Open an issue from [Issues](/docs/issues), or select **Open full page** for more room.

## Read the overview

The top of the issue shows its summary, severity and status. **Overview** then tells you:

- What’s affected, such as a SKU, an order or a connection.
- **Why it matters**: the potential exposure, such as units that could be oversold. Potential exposure doesn’t confirm lost sales or oversold orders. If Luma can’t establish the impact yet, it says the impact is unknown.
- **Next action**: what to do now. If Luma proposed a change, select **Review proposed change**. If it can’t fix the problem itself, it says so and lists the manual steps.
- **Steps and checks**: what to confirm **Before you continue**, and the **Expected result and how Luma checks it**.

On the full page, the section list at the side moves through **Overview**, **Findings**, **Change and outcome check**, **History**, **Evidence**, **Proposed change** and **Issue details**. On a narrow screen, choose a section from **On this page**.

## Check findings and evidence

**Findings** separates what Luma knows from what it suspects:

- **Confirmed facts** are backed by data Luma read.
- **What remains uncertain** and **Possible causes and checks needed** list explanations Luma hasn’t confirmed, and what would confirm them.
- **Contradicting evidence** shows data that doesn’t fit the conclusion.

Each finding links to its evidence. **Evidence** lists the reads behind the issue: which system and record each came from, when Luma read it, when the record last changed in the source, and whether the read was complete. The issue shows the latest three; the button below them, such as **View all 12 evidence records**, opens the full list.

## When evidence is out of date or the state isn’t confirmed

Luma labels findings whose data may no longer reflect your systems:

- **Evidence is out of date**: the data is too old to act on. Luma needs fresh data before you choose a fix. Its recommendations may no longer apply.
- **Investigation in progress**: Luma is still reading data. Findings and next steps appear when it finishes.
- **More evidence needed**: linked records, the leading system or the data Luma read don’t support a firm conclusion yet.
- **Investigation couldn’t finish**: there’s no recommendation yet. Select **Check for updates** later.
- **Investigation not confirmed** or **Current state not confirmed**: Luma lost its live connection to the issue, for example while you’re offline. You see the last confirmed state. Reconnect or refresh before you act.

Next to **Change and outcome check**, **Live** means the issue updates on its own. **Live updates interrupted** or **Connection lost** means you may be looking at an older state.

## Follow an issue to its result

An issue moves through five stages: **Investigation**, **Approval**, **Change**, **Check** and **Result**. Its status shows where it is, for example **Needs approval**, **Sending change** or **Checking outcome**.

**Change and outcome check** keeps three things apart:

1. Whether a change was sent, for example **Waiting for approval**, **Change queued** or **No change sent**.
2. Whether the connected system accepted it: **Change accepted**, **Change failed** or **Outcome unclear**.
3. Whether a fresh read confirmed the result: **Outcome confirmed** or **Outcome not confirmed**.

An accepted change doesn’t prove the problem is fixed. Only **Resolved and confirmed** means a fresh check confirmed the fix. See [Confirm the outcome](/docs/issues/resolve#confirm-the-outcome).

## Look at related records and connections

On the full page, links next to **Issues** at the top open the records behind the issue:

- **Inspect inventory** and **Inspect product** for a SKU.
- **Inspect fulfillment** for a delayed fulfillment.
- **Open connection** for a connection issue. If the connection lost access, the link reads **Reconnect** followed by its name, such as **Reconnect My shop**.
- **Find records** searches [Records](/docs/records) for the affected record.

**Issue details** lists the source systems, data freshness, affected record, when Luma first detected the issue and its last activity. Select **Copy issue ID** to share the ID with your team.

## Read the history

**History** lists everything that happened, newest first: when the issue was detected, approvals, rejections, escalations, changes sent and checks. Each entry says who did it. Select **View full history** to see every entry and filter them by **Event type** or **Outcome**.

If history can’t load, the rest of the issue still works. Select **Retry history**.

## Next steps

To approve, reject or escalate a proposed change, see [Resolve an issue](/docs/issues/resolve). To see what a workflow would do with this issue without changing anything, open **Test a Workflow on this issue**; see [Test a workflow safely](/docs/shadow-mode).
