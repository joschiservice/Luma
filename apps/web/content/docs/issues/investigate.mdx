---
title: Investigate an issue
description: Understand the problem, check the evidence, and choose your next step.
---

An investigation combines the detected problem with findings from connected systems. Read the overview first, then check the evidence that supports the recommended next step.

## Understand the overview

Check the issue summary, affected records, and source systems. Severity helps you prioritize; the issue state tells you where work stands. A need for review means there is something for a person to assess.

Use the section navigation on the full issue page to move through the investigation. On smaller screens, choose a section from **On this page**.

## Check findings and evidence

Read the explanation and supporting observations together. Check when the evidence was observed and whether it is still current. Missing or outdated evidence can prevent a reliable diagnosis or correction.

The issue details also identify the issue type, source systems, and evidence freshness. Expand supporting records when you need to trace a finding to its source.

Use **Refresh** if the page reports that the investigation could not be confirmed. If an issue is unavailable, it may have been removed or your access may have changed.

## Choose the next step

Follow the recommended next step in the investigation. Depending on the issue, that may involve a proposed correction, manual work, or escalation.

When a proposed correction is available, read [Review a resolution](/docs/issues/resolve) before making a decision. Check the target system, the proposed changes, and the expected result.

An absent or disabled action can reflect your permissions, the current evidence, policy restrictions, or an action that is no longer available. Read the message beside the action for the reason.

## Follow progress

Execution and verification are separate stages. A recorded approval means a decision was accepted; it does not prove the correction succeeded.

Check execution status and the verification result. **Resolution verified** means fresh evidence confirmed the correction. **Closed by human disposition** records a human closure without establishing a verified correction.

## Return to the inbox

Use **Back to issues** on the full page, or close the selected issue in the inbox. Your inbox view and filters are preserved.

See [Find and prioritize issues](/docs/issues) for views, filters, and empty states.
