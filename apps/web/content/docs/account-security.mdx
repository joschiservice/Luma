---
title: Secure your sign-in
description: Add an authenticator app code to your password, keep backup codes, and sign in with a passkey.
---

Your sign-in protects every workspace you belong to, including approvals and changes to connected systems. Open **Settings → Security** to add a second step after your password, or a passkey that replaces the password. These settings belong to your account, not to one workspace.

## Turn on two-factor authentication

With two-factor authentication on, Luma asks for a 6-digit code from an authenticator app after your password. Any app that supports time-based codes works, such as 1Password, Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator.

1. Under **Authenticator app**, select **Set up** and enter your password.
2. Save the 10 backup codes somewhere safe, such as your password manager. You won't see them again.
3. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, or enter the setup key.
4. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows and select **Turn on**.

Two-factor authentication starts only once the code is confirmed. If you close the dialog earlier, nothing changes.

If a code is rejected, check that your device sets its time automatically. Codes change every 30 seconds.

## Sign in with a code

After your email and password, enter the current code from your authenticator app. If you don't have your device, select **Use a backup code**. Each backup code works once.

After five incorrect codes, sign in with your password again. After repeated failures, Luma locks code entry for 15 minutes.

## Replace your backup codes

Select **New backup codes** and enter your password. Your previous codes stop working immediately. Do this if you've used most of your codes or think someone else has seen them.

## Use a passkey

A passkey signs you in with your fingerprint, face, device PIN or a security key. It can't be phished or reused on another site. Signing in with a passkey skips both your password and the code step.

1. Under **Passkeys**, optionally name the passkey, for example "Work laptop".
2. Select **Add passkey** and follow your device's prompt.

On the sign-in page, select **Sign in with a passkey**. Add a passkey on each device you use, unless your password manager syncs passkeys between devices. The list shows whether each passkey is synced or tied to one device.

To add a passkey, you need to have signed in within the last day. If Luma asks you to sign in again, sign out and back in, then add the passkey.

## Remove a passkey or turn off two-factor authentication

Select **Remove** next to a passkey. Also delete it from your device or password manager.

To stop using codes, select **Turn off** and enter your password. Your backup codes stop working. Passkeys you've added keep working.

## If you lose access

If you lose your authenticator device, sign in with a backup code or a passkey, then turn two-factor authentication off and set it up again on your new device. Without a backup code or a passkey, you can't finish signing in, so keep at least one of them available.
