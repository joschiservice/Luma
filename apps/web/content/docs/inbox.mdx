title: Work supplier and customer email

The **Inbox** brings the operational email of a shared mailbox, such as orders@ or purchasing@, into Luma. Each thread shows who owns it, when a reply is due and which orders, customers, suppliers or purchase orders it's about. Open it from the sidebar, or press **G** then **E**.

## Connect a mailbox

1. Open **Manage apps** and connect **Gmail** with the shared mailbox's Google account.
2. Open **Inbox**, then **Mailboxes**, and add the rules that decide what Luma reads.

Until a mailbox has at least one rule, Luma reads nothing from it. A mailbox can belong to one workspace only.

## Choose what Luma reads

Luma reads only messages that match a rule, and the replies your team sends to the same correspondents. Everything else in the mailbox stays unread by Luma. Add a rule for:

- **Sender domain**: every message from a supplier's domain, such as `northstar.example`. Subdomains match too.
- **Sender address**: one address, such as `orders@northstar.example`.
- **Recipient address (alias)**: messages sent to an address that arrives in this mailbox, such as `purchasing@yourshop.example`.
- **Label**: messages with a label or in a folder of the mailbox.

Each rule says who the correspondent is: a **supplier** you choose, **customers** or **other**. Rules for a supplier link that supplier's threads automatically. When you add a rule, Luma also reads matching messages from the **read back** period, 30 days unless you change it. Removing a rule stops new reads; threads already read stay.

**Mailboxes** shows whether each mailbox is up to date, when it was last read and how far its messages are covered. If reading is **Blocked**, for example because Google access was revoked, reconnect the mailbox in **Manage apps**. **Pause reading** stops reading until you resume.

Message text is kept for a limited time and then removed; sender, time, links and reviewed facts remain. People who can work on threads can also remove a message's text sooner from **Source details**.

## Work a thread

Choose a view: **Open**, **Mine**, **Unassigned**, **Overdue**, **To review** (document facts waiting for review), **Closed** or **All**. The tabs also include the views you and your team saved, and opening the inbox from the sidebar returns to the view you used last. Search by subject or sender; search isn't saved with a view.

**Owner** and **Mailbox** above the list narrow it in one click. **Owner** finds threads you own, threads nobody owns or the threads of one person; a view saved with **Me** shows each person their own threads. For anything else, choose **Filter**: **Status**, **Reply deadline**, **Last message**, **Linked record type**, **Has attachments**, **Awaiting reply** or **Facts to review**. Filters use thread properties only, never message text. The numbers on the views count every thread in each view, whatever you filter.

The newest activity comes first. The sort menu after **Display** sorts by last message or reply deadline, either way; threads without a deadline come last. Select **Display** to choose what each thread shows, such as the subject, owner, status, mailbox, reply deadline or last message, to group threads by mailbox, owner or status, and to switch to **Compact** density. Reply deadlines, awaiting replies, facts to review and attachments always show. Changed filters, sorting, grouping or display mark the view **Modified**; select **Save**, **Save as new view** or **Reset**. Density and collapsed groups are your own settings.

Work the list from the keyboard: **J** and **K**, or the arrow keys, move through it; **Space** opens the thread you're on, or closes it, and while it's open **J** and **K** open the next and previous thread; **Esc** closes the thread and returns you to its row; **/** jumps to search; **F** adds a filter; **?** lists these shortcuts.

- **Owner**: assign the thread to a teammate, or take it yourself.
- **Status**: **Open**, **Waiting** for the other side, or **Closed**. A new message reopens a closed thread.
- **Reply deadline**: Luma sets a deadline after an unanswered message, 24 hours unless the mailbox says otherwise. Set your own deadline, or return to **Automatic**. Overdue threads are labeled **Reply overdue**, and their owner finds them in [Needs you](/docs/needs-you).
- **Internal notes**: notes for your team. They stay in Luma and are never sent.

The timeline records who changed what and when.

### Sender verification

Gmail checks whether a received message really comes from the domain in its From address. When that check fails, the message shows **Sender not verified**: the From address may be forged, so check with the sender, for example by phone, before you act on it. When Gmail recorded no check, as for mail imported into the mailbox, the message shows **Sender couldn't be verified**. Messages from verified senders show no label. Mail from colleagues on your own Google Workspace domain counts as verified when Google signed it for that domain.

Luma never treats an unverified sender's message as proof: supplier commitments from it wait for your review, and it doesn't confirm that an order email arrived.

## Linked records

Luma links a thread when the evidence is exact: a supplier rule, a customer's email address, or an order or purchase order number in the message. Each link shows how it was found. A reference that matches several records is only **Suggested**; confirm the right one or dismiss it. A supplier rule or customer address that matched a sender Luma couldn't verify is also only **Suggested**, because anyone can write someone else's address in the From line. Use **Link record** to add a link yourself, and **Remove link** to remove one; Luma doesn't add a removed link again.

Linked records show their threads on their own page under **Inbox threads**. A supplier message linked to exactly one purchase order is also recorded on that purchase order, where its commitments are reviewed.

## Review documents

Luma reads PDF, spreadsheet, CSV and text attachments up to 10 MB. Open an attachment to see the document beside the facts Luma proposes from it: parties, references, lines, dates and amounts. Select a fact to highlight the text or cells it comes from.

- **Confirm** a fact that's correct, **Correct** it, or **Reject** it.
- Facts with **Low confidence**, or that match several records, need a correction before you can confirm them; choose the right record while correcting.
- Select text or cells (Shift-click for a range) and choose **Add fact** to add one yourself, for example when Luma doesn't propose facts for a document.
- When a new version of a file arrives, facts show whether they're **New** or **Changed**, and facts no longer in the file are listed under **Not in this version**.

A file that arrived before is marked **Duplicate**; review it on the original. Confirmed facts are proposals for later work. They never change an order or send anything by themselves.

## Reply and approval

Choose **Draft reply** to get a reply built from the current state of the linked records. **Sources** lists each record the draft relies on, and when it was read. Information older than a day or that couldn't be read is stated in the draft, never filled in. Edit the recipients, subject and text, then save your changes.

- A reply that confirms a date or a quantity, or mentions money, needs approval from someone else; so does every reply when the mailbox requires approval for all replies. The reply says why. Choose **Request approval**; the approver finds it in Needs you or on the thread, and you send it once it's approved.
- Other replies are sent when you choose **Send**.

A reply counts as **Sent and confirmed** only after Luma reads the thread again and finds it in the mailbox. If Luma can't tell whether the mailbox sent a reply, it shows **Outcome unknown**. Choose **Check the mailbox**: Luma only reads the thread to find out and never sends the reply a second time.

Open **Send evidence** on a sent reply to see the mailbox’s receipt and the message Luma found when it checked the thread. See [Read the evidence](/docs/issues/resolve#read-the-evidence).
