---
title: Manage workspace settings
description: Change the workspace name and time zone, invite and manage your team, set approval rules, and choose your notifications, language and layout.
---

**Settings** has two groups. **Workspace** settings are shared by everyone in the workspace: **Workspace**, **Team**, **Roles**, **Approvals** and **Luma support**. **Personal** settings apply only to you: **Security**, **Notifications**, **Language & appearance** and **Home & sidebar**. Press **G** then **S** to open Settings.

Everyone can view the workspace sections. If your role can’t change one, it says which permission you need.

## Workspace

Change the **Workspace name** and **Time zone**, then select **Save changes**. You need **Edit workspace settings**. The name appears in navigation and the workspace switcher. Luma shows dates and times in this workspace in its time zone by default. Under **Language & appearance**, you can choose a display time zone for this device or restore **Use workspace time zone**. Times include the zone; point to a time to see the exact moment.

The **Workspace identifier** and **Data region** can’t be changed. Workspace data is stored in the region shown here. Australian workspaces use Australia (Sydney). Luma processes workspace data in its region; connected providers and services you authorize may process it elsewhere.

If Luma couldn’t confirm whether a change was saved, select **Check status and retry** before you change anything else.

Under **Issue detection**, set stock tolerance and confirmation windows in seconds. Oversell risk has its own shorter window. You can also set how long fulfillment can go without progress. Existing issue details show the limits used to detect them.

## Team

**Team** lists everyone with access to this workspace and their role, 25 people per page. Search by name, email or role to find someone. Inviting people and changing their access needs **Manage team members**.

### Invite someone

1. Select **Invite teammate**.
2. Enter their email address and choose a role.
3. Select **Send invitation**.

Luma emails a personal link. Access starts when the person accepts, signed in with that email address. Invitations can’t make someone an owner; an owner can change their role after they join.

Open invitations appear under **Invitations** with their status, such as **Email sent. Waiting for them to accept.** or **Email not delivered. Resend to try again.** Invitations expire after 7 days.

- **Resend** sends a new link. The earlier link stops working.
- **Revoke** cancels the invitation. The link stops working immediately.
- **Invite again** sends a new invitation after one expired.

If this Luma server isn’t set up to send email, you can’t invite people. Ask whoever runs Luma for you to set up email delivery.

### Change someone’s role or remove access

Select **⋯** next to the person to open **Manage access**. Choose a role and select **Save role**, or select **Remove access**.

- A new role applies from the person’s next action in Luma.
- You can only assign roles whose permissions your own role includes.
- Only owners can make someone an owner or change another owner.
- Every workspace needs an owner. Make someone else an owner before you change or remove the only one.

### Suspended access

Luma support can suspend someone’s access to this workspace, for example when an employee leaves and no owner is available. The person shows **Access suspended** in **Team**, keeps their role and can’t open the workspace until their access is restored. **Activity** shows the change by **Luma support**.

Workflows run with the access of the person who turned them on. While that person’s access is suspended, their workflows pause and show that they need a new owner. Turn the workflow on again, or set up the stock rule again, to run it with your access.

If every owner’s access is suspended, **Team** says that the workspace has no active owner. Contact Luma support to transfer ownership.

## Roles

**Roles** shows what each role allows and lets you create custom roles. See [Roles and permissions](/docs/roles-and-permissions).

## Approvals

For **Replenishment purchase orders**, one approval can cover creating the draft and sending it unchanged to the reviewed supplier connection. The requester chooses the delivery date when sending. Turn on **Always review sending separately** to require two approvals. You can instead set an amount and currency for separate send review at or above that amount. Orders with unknown prices or another currency need separate review when this threshold is set.

**Approvals** decides when changes need review and who may approve them. Changing these settings needs **Manage approval rules**.

- **Standard approval rules**: low-risk changes that people are permitted to make go ahead. Higher-risk changes need approval.
- **Always require approval**: every otherwise permitted change needs approval, including low-risk changes.
- **Require approval from another person**: when on, requesters cannot approve their own changes. When off, a requester with **Approve or reject changes** can approve their own changes.

Requiring another person is off while only one active member has workspace access. Pending invitations, suspended members and disabled accounts do not count. When another member gains access, the team preference applies again; it starts on unless you have turned it off.

Permissions, required reviews and a fresh check of the outcome still apply. Changing either setting, or gaining or losing the second active member, means open reviews must be refreshed before their changes can run.

### Delegate internal follow-ups

If **Delegated follow-ups** is available under **Approvals**, create a policy for the suppliers, receiving locations or purchase orders Luma may follow up. An empty selection covers every eligible record in that dimension. Set a daily note limit and give a review reason. New policies start in **Manual** mode.

- **Manual** lets you handle the responsibility yourself.
- **Assisted** asks for a decision before Luma records an internal follow-up. Open **Needs you** to review the exact note and order.
- **Autonomous** permits eligible internal notes within reviewed limits. It requires explicit promotion after enough live reviews and independent verification. Synthetic and unknown evidence do not earn this authority. Other approval rules still apply.

These notes are recorded in Luma; they do not send a supplier message or place an order. Daily limits use UTC and include reserved attempts. Optional money limits block work when the amount or currency is unknown.

Choose **Preview recorded work** to see how the current policy treats retained samples, with live, synthetic and unknown results labelled separately. A preview does not execute anything or prove production reliability. Pause the scope or internal connection to prevent new work. Failed or uncertain verification also pauses the policy and lowers its mode; review the evidence before resuming.

When order inputs or policy change, refresh the proposal in **Needs you** and review a new decision. Earlier approval does not transfer. Verify an uncertain existing attempt before retrying; verification does not send another note.

### Review operational source use

In **Settings → Approvals**, connection managers can review **Source operational use**. Select **Read source evidence** for the connection you use for real operations. Review the authenticated source, its revisions and the read time. The review expires after five minutes.

Confirm that you reviewed the source and that it contains real operational commerce data. Add a reason, then select **Save reviewed live declaration**. This is a reviewed workspace declaration, not provider certification. It does not grant write permission or enable Autonomous mode. Synthetic sources cannot be declared live, and unknown or synthetic history cannot earn autonomous authority.

If a save response is lost, select **Check saved declaration** before preparing another change. Luma retries the same reviewed command. If the connection or credentials change, source use becomes unknown until a fresh review and declaration. Earlier history keeps its original classification.

To stop using a declaration, add a reason and select **Revoke live declaration**. Revocation prevents the declaration from establishing live source use for new work; it does not rewrite earlier history or undo dispatched actions.

## Luma support

**Luma support** decides whether Luma support can look into a problem by seeing this workspace as one of your members. Changing it needs **Manage Luma support access**.

- Choose how long to allow access, **24 hours**, **3 days** or **7 days**, and select **Allow access**. **Set new end time** replaces the current end time.
- **Revoke access** ends it now, including any view that is open.
- When Luma support asks for access, the request appears here with their reason. **Approve** allows access for the requested time; **Decline** refuses it. Owners also get an email about the request.

A support view is read-only: support sees exactly what that member sees, and Luma refuses every change. Each view ends after 30 minutes. Owners get an email when a view starts, and **Activity** shows every view, each page it opened, and when it ended, by **Luma support**.

For a security incident or legal request, Luma support can open a view without your approval. Owners get an email when it ends, and it appears in **Activity**.

## Security

**Security** holds your sign-in settings: two-factor authentication, backup codes and passkeys. They apply to your account in every workspace. See [Secure your sign-in](/docs/account-security).

## Notifications

Choose which emails Luma sends you about this workspace:

- **Issues that need you**: an issue needs your approval, a manual step or a look at evidence Luma couldn’t confirm.
- **Connection problems**: a connected system isn’t responding reliably or its access was revoked.

Turning a notification on doesn’t send emails about earlier events. Needs you and Home always show work that needs your attention, whatever you choose here.

Under **Test email delivery**, select **Send test email** to check that emails reach you. **Test email accepted for delivery** means the mail server took the email, not that it arrived; it can take a few minutes to reach your inbox. If you see **Email delivery isn’t set up**, this Luma server can’t send email yet. Your choices are saved and apply once someone sets it up.

If you see **Email to you is paused**, Luma’s email to you bounced or was marked as spam, so Luma stopped sending notifications. Invitations and account and security emails still go out. Fix the address with your email provider or mark Luma’s email as not spam, then send a test email. When it arrives, notifications resume.

## Language & appearance

Choose the **Language** and **Theme** (**System**, **Light** or **Dark**), and whether to **Collapse the sidebar**. These choices apply to this device. Luma reloads the page to switch the language. Emails from Luma, such as notifications and account notices, use the language you chose last. See [Change the language](/docs/navigation#change-the-language).

## Home & sidebar

Choose which **Home modules** appear, in which order and at which width, or select **Reset to default**. Under **Pinned pages**, rename, reorder or unpin the pages in your sidebar; you can pin up to 12. Only you see these choices, and they belong to this workspace. See [Customize Home](/docs/home#customize-home) and [Pin a page](/docs/navigation#pin-a-page).

## Source authority

Open **Settings → Source authority** to review which connection supplies each order fact and inventory quantity. A storefront supplies order lines and lifecycle by default; an ERP supplies fulfillment, tracking and stock. Order groups can use the other category when only that source is available.

To change a fact group, choose a connection under **Workspace override**, then choose **Save**. Choose **Use category default** to restore the default. You need **Edit workspace settings** to change these choices. Every change is recorded and applies to the next detection run without changing earlier source evidence.

**Unknown authority** means no eligible source can supply the fact, or an override is unavailable. **Conflicting authority** means several connections match the default. Select a connection to resolve it. Automatic stock corrections remain blocked while authority is unknown or conflicting.
