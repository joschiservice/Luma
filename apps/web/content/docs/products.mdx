---
title: Compare products across systems
description: Find a SKU, see how your store and ERP list it, and spot listings that are missing, disagree or should be linked.
---

Products shows every SKU that a connected store or ERP lists in its catalog. Each SKU has one row with every source side by side, so you can see how Shopify and your ERP describe the same item. Luma reads catalogs; it doesn’t change or create listings. Open **Products** in the sidebar or press **G**, then **K**.

## Find a SKU

Search by SKU code, product or variant title, barcode, vendor, or the product or SKU ID in a source. Choose a view:

- **All SKUs** shows every SKU in a connected catalog.
- **Differs by source** shows SKUs whose sources disagree on title, status, sales channel, price or barcode.
- **Not in every source** shows SKUs that at least one catalog doesn’t list. Some items are sold or managed in one system only on purpose, such as packaging kept only in your ERP.
- **Possible matches** shows SKUs with a SKU of the same code in another source that isn’t linked yet.

The tabs above the list show how many SKUs each view holds under your search and filters; the view menu also lists the views you and your team saved. Opening **Products** from the sidebar returns to the view you used last.

**Listed in** and **Status** above the list narrow it in one click. For anything else, choose **Filter** and pick a field, such as **Missing from**, **Differs in**, **Vendor**, **Price**, **Barcode**, **Sales channel**, **Bundle**, **Updated** or a custom field. Listing fields such as status, vendor and price let you choose a **Source**: **Any source** matches when one listing matches, **Every source** needs every listing to match, and a named source reads only that source's listings. Prices compare only within the condition's currency. The sort menu starts with **Catalog order**; choose it to sort by SKU, title, price, last update, differing fields, number of listings or a custom field, or flip the direction. SKUs without the sorted value come last.

## Arrange and save the list

Choose **Display** to switch between **Table** and **List**, choose columns, and group SKUs by vendor, status or differing field. A SKU with listings in two statuses appears in both groups. Each catalog source has its own listing column, such as **Listing in JTL-Wawi**, so you can show only the sources you compare. Custom product fields can be columns too. You see only the custom fields your role can read; a SKU without a value shows **—** and matches **is empty**. A custom field that was retired shows as archived, and a view that filters or sorts by it, or by a field your role can’t read, shows a notice: choose **Remove unsupported conditions** to see the list. **Open records in a preview** shows a SKU's listings, stock and recent sales beside the list.

When you change filters, sorting, grouping or display, the view is marked **Modified**. Choose **Save as new view** to keep it; see [Saved views](/docs/saved-views). **J** and **K** move through the SKUs, **Enter** opens one, **Space** previews it, **/** goes to search, **F** adds a filter and **?** lists every shortcut.

**Coverage** above the list shows how much of each catalog Luma has imported. **No products imported yet** means no connected store or ERP has shared its catalog; check [Data sync](/docs/data-sync) for progress.

## How sources are compared

Luma doesn’t treat any source as the authority. It compares what each source reports:

- **Title** is compared without regard to case, spacing and punctuation, so “Linen apron · Small” and “Linen apron Small” agree.
- **Price** is compared only within the same currency. Prices without a currency, such as JTL-Wawi item prices, are shown but not compared.
- **Barcode** is compared without leading zeros, so the UPC and EAN forms of one code agree.
- **Sales channel** means publication to the Shopify Online Store, or activation for shops in JTL-Wawi.

Whether a source lists a SKU is only certain after Luma has read that source’s full catalog. Until then, the product page says Luma hasn’t read the full catalog yet.

## Read a product

The comparison table lines up each source’s product, SKU code, status, sales channel, price, barcode, vendor, source IDs and freshness. **Differs** marks the fields that disagree. **Not in every source** names each source that doesn’t list the SKU. If it should be sold or managed there, add it in that system.

Below the comparison you find what depends on the SKU:

- **Stock by location**, with each source’s available quantity. **Open in Inventory** compares stock at a location. See [Inventory](/docs/inventory).
- **Sales in the last 30 days** per source, as units ordered by date placed minus reported cancellations. Sales aren’t added across sources, because the same order can exist in more than one system.
- **Orders with this SKU**, open orders first. **Search orders for** the SKU opens every order that mentions its code.
- **Supply commitments** from purchase orders, which aren’t confirmed incoming stock.
- **Issues** for the SKU or its stock, and **Other variants** of the same product.
- **Linked sources**, the source records Luma treats as this SKU.
- **Listing history**, each catalog reading Luma recorded, so you can see when a price or status changed.

You can also reach a product from an order line, a stock position, an issue about a SKU, **Records** and search.

## Link the same SKU across sources

Luma treats records from different sources as the same SKU only after they’re linked, by a person or by an [automatic linking](/docs/data-sync#automatic-linking) rule. When a store and an ERP use the same code for one item, the row shows **Possible match to link**.

1. On the product page, go to **Linked sources**. **Possibly the same SKU** lists SKUs in other sources with the same code.
2. Choose **Link** beside the match.
3. Check the reason under **Why is this correct?** and change it if needed, then choose **Link records**.

Once the link is done, both listings appear in one row, and stock, products and order lines from both sources belong to the same SKU. Each source keeps its own quantities. To separate a source again, choose **Unlink** beside it, then **Unlink source**. Every link and unlink is recorded with who made it and why. For more about linking stock, see [Link the same SKU across systems](/docs/inventory#link-the-same-sku-across-systems).

## Who can see and link products

Viewing products needs **View products**. Linking and unlinking SKUs needs **Manage imports**; without it, you see the linked sources and possible matches but not **Link** or **Unlink**.
