---
title: Ask Luma
description: Ask questions about your orders, issues, stock, fulfillment and returns in plain language, and check each answer against the records it came from.
---

**Ask Luma** answers questions about your workspace in plain language, such as “Which orders from last week are still unfulfilled?” or “Which shipments are stalled?”. Each answer links to the records it used, so you can check it before you act.

## Open Ask Luma

- Select **Ask Luma** in the top bar.
- Press ⌘ J on a Mac or Ctrl J on Windows and Linux. This works even while you’re typing in a field.
- In the command menu (⌘ K or Ctrl K), type a question of 3 characters or more and choose **Ask Luma**.

Press the same keys, select **Close Ask Luma** or press Esc in the panel to close it. The panel stays open beside the page while you move around the workspace. On a narrow screen it covers the page, and opening a linked record closes it.

Type your question and press Enter, or choose one of the **Suggested questions**. Select **Stop answering** to cancel; nothing is changed.

## What Ask Luma can answer

Ask Luma reads current records for every question:

- **Orders**: find orders and see an order’s lines, fulfillments, returns, issues and workflow runs.
- **Issues**: what needs attention, and an issue’s findings, possible causes and next steps.
- **Stock**: on hand, reserved, available and incoming quantities in each system.
- **Fulfillment**: held, delayed, stalled and in-transit work.
- **Returns**: returns and refunds by stage, with the amount actually refunded.

It reads with your access. It sees only what your role lets you see on the matching pages, and nothing from other workspaces. If your role doesn’t include orders, for example, it can’t read orders for you. If your access changes while it’s answering, it stops.

Customer contact and payment details stay in your connected systems. Ask Luma can’t read them.

## Check an answer

Answers can be wrong. Before you act on one, open the records it links to.

- Numbers and names in an answer link to the records they came from. **Sources** lists each record with its system and when Luma last read it. Select a record to open its page.
- The line above the answer, such as **2 reads · 14 records**, opens the list of lookups Luma made. A lookup marked **Couldn’t read** failed, and the answer should say what it couldn’t check.
- **As of** shows when the answer was written.

Ask Luma says so when systems disagree, when data is out of date or when a list was cut short.

## Ask a follow-up

Ask a follow-up in the same conversation, for example “Which of those is the oldest?”. Ask Luma reads the records again instead of relying on its earlier answer. **New conversation** starts over.

Conversations aren’t saved. They stay in this browser tab until you start a new one or reload the page.

## Limits

- Ask Luma only reads. It can’t change records, approve changes, retry anything or start workflows. To act, open the linked record and use its controls.
- Each question can make a limited number of lookups and takes up to about a minute. When Ask Luma reaches a limit, the answer is marked **Incomplete**. Ask a narrower question, for example with a shorter date range, one status or one order number.
- Questions can be up to 2,000 characters.

## Who can use Ask Luma

Your role needs **Use Ask Luma**. Without it, the panel says your role doesn’t include Ask Luma; ask a workspace admin for access.

If Ask Luma isn’t available in your workspace, ask your administrator.
