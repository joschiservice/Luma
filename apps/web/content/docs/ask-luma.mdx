---
title: Ask Luma
description: Ask questions about your orders, issues, stock, fulfillment and returns in plain language, and follow each answer to the records it came from.
---

**Ask Luma** answers questions about your workspace, such as "Which orders from last week are still unfulfilled?" or "Which shipments are stalled?". Open it with **Ask Luma** in the top bar or press ⌘ J (Ctrl J on Windows and Linux). Press the same keys, or Esc while you're in the panel, to close it. You can also type a question in the command menu (⌘ K) and choose **Ask Luma**.

The panel stays open beside the page while you move around the workspace. On small screens it covers the page, and opening a linked record closes it.

## What Luma reads

Luma looks up current records for every question:

- **Orders**: search, the outstanding, overdue, needs-you and sources-disagree views, and an order's lines, fulfillments, returns, issues and workflow runs.
- **Issues**: the Issues inbox views, and an issue's diagnosis with its likely causes and next steps.
- **Stock**: each source's on-hand, reserved, available and incoming quantities.
- **Fulfillment**: held, delayed, stalled and in-transit work.
- **Returns**: returns and refunds by stage, with the money actually returned.

Luma uses your access. It sees exactly what you can see on the matching pages, and nothing from other workspaces. If your access changes while it's answering, it stops.

Customer contact and payment details stay in the connected apps. Luma can't read them.

## Check the answer

Numbers in an answer link to the records they came from. **Sources** lists each one, with the source system and when Luma last read it. Select a record to open its page.

Select the read summary above an answer, such as **2 reads · 14 records**, to see each lookup Luma made. A read marked **Couldn't read** failed. The answer should say what it couldn't check.

Answers come from a language model and can be wrong. Before you act, check the linked records. Luma says so when sources disagree, data is stale or a list was cut short.

## Follow-up questions

Ask a follow-up in the same conversation, for example "Which of those is the oldest?". Luma reads the records again instead of relying on its earlier answer. **New conversation** starts over.

Conversations aren't saved. They stay in this browser tab until you start a new one or reload the page.

## Limits

Luma only reads. It can't change records, approve, retry or start workflows. To act on something, open the cited record and use its controls with your own permissions.

Each question has a limit on lookups, answer length and time, about a minute. When Luma reaches a limit, the answer is marked **Incomplete**. Ask a narrower question, for example with a shorter date range, one status or an order number.

## When Ask Luma isn't available

Ask Luma needs a model service that processes data in the EU and doesn't keep it. If the server doesn't have one, the panel says what's missing and nothing is sent. Whoever runs your Luma server can set it up.
