---
title: Find and read orders
description: Find an order, see what still needs work, compare what each connected system reports, and link a Shopify order with its JTL-Wawi order.
---

Orders lists every current order your connected stores and ERPs report. Use it to find orders that still need work, see where systems disagree about an order, and follow an order to its issues, fulfillments and returns. Open **Orders** in the sidebar or press **G**, then **O**.

## Find an order

Choose a view in the view menu above the list:

- **All orders** shows every current order from your connected systems.
- **Outstanding** shows open orders with units still to fulfill.
- **Overdue** shows outstanding orders past the deadline set in their source.
- **Needs you** shows orders linked to an issue that needs your decision or review.
- **Differs by source** shows orders whose status, payment, total or ordered items differ between sources.
- **Journey checks** finds paid open orders without a recorded Luma reservation after four hours, and shipments without one. Investigate these hints with the inventory team and the reporting source. External reservations may be unread. This view requires inventory and fulfillment access.

The view menu also lists the views you and your team saved. Opening **Orders** from the sidebar returns to the view you used last, with any changes you hadn't saved.

Search by order number, the order’s ID in its source or a SKU on an order line. When you can view customers, search also finds orders by the customer’s name or email address, so your search text stays on the page: it isn’t saved in the page address or in views, and it’s cleared when you leave or reload the page. A link that opens Orders with a search, such as a product’s orders, fills in the search and then removes it from the address. **Status**, **Payment**, **Source** and **Warehouse** above the list narrow it in one click. For anything else, choose **Filter** and pick a field, such as **Placed**, **Lines**, **Total**, **Sales channel**, **Tags** or a custom field, then a condition and a value. When two or more filters apply, choose whether orders must match all of them or any. The sort menu after **Display** names the current order, such as **Updated**; choose it to sort by another field or flip the direction. In the table, a column header sorts by that column, and its menu offers **Filter by this column**. **Clear** removes every filter and the search.

Outstanding, Overdue and Needs you count the same orders as the order counts on Home and in [Insights](/docs/insights). Choosing a count there opens the matching view here, with the same filters.

**Coverage** above the list says whether the list is complete, for example while older history is still importing or when a source shares only recent orders. See [Data sync](/docs/data-sync).

**No orders match** means the search, view or filters exclude every order. **No orders yet** means no connected store or ERP has reported an order. If a page link no longer matches its filters, choose **First page**. When a view uses a filter or grouping you can't use, for example because your role can't see warehouses, Luma says so instead of showing more orders than the view means; choose **Remove unsupported conditions**.

## Arrange the list

Choose **Display** to change how the list looks:

- **Layout**: **Table** shows columns, **List** shows one row per order with the properties you choose, and **Board** shows a column per group.
- **Columns** or **Properties**: choose what each order shows and drag them into order. In the table you can also resize, move, pin and hide columns from each column’s menu.
- **Group by**: group orders by status, payment, fulfillment, source, warehouse, sales channel, tags or other fields, and dates by day, week or month. Each group shows how many orders it has and the total per currency. **Incomplete** means the count may be missing orders. Choose a group’s name to collapse it, and **Show more** to see more of its orders. **Show empty groups** lists statuses and other choices that no order has.
- **Density**: **Compact** fits more orders on screen. Density, column widths and collapsed groups are your own settings.
- **Open records in a preview**: choosing an order shows its summary beside the list instead of opening it: its current state, the first lines with the units still to fulfill, and the issues still open on it. Point at or tab to a status, payment, fulfillment or source to narrow the list to that value, or hide it. **Open full order** opens the order.

On the board you can’t move orders between columns, because their status, payment and fulfillment come from your connected systems.

## Save and share views

When you change filters, sorting, grouping or display, the view is marked **Modified**. Choose **Save** to update a saved view you can edit, **Save as new view** to keep your changes as a new view, or **Reset** to return to the view as it was saved. A new view is private unless you share it with your workspace or with roles; see [Saved views](/docs/saved-views). Sharing a view never gives anyone access to orders they can't see.

If someone else saved the view after you opened it, Luma keeps your changes and lets you save them as a new view or load theirs instead.

The view and your changes are part of the page address, so you can copy the link or pin the view to the sidebar from **View options**. **Make my default for this page** opens that view when you come to Orders. **Reset page to default** clears what Orders remembers for you; choose **Undo** to bring it back.

## Work from the keyboard

- **J** and **K**, or the arrow keys, move through the orders. On a board, the arrow keys also move between columns.
- **Enter** opens the order you're on.
- **Space** opens or closes it in the preview, when the preview is on. While it's open, **J** and **K** show the next and previous order.
- **X** selects an order, **Shift**+**X** selects a range, and **Esc** clears the selection or closes the preview.
- **/** goes to search, **F** adds a filter and **?** lists these shortcuts.

Shortcuts don't work while you type in a field.

## Read an order

The order number appears in the bar at the top, after **Orders**. Below it are the order’s status, payment, fulfillment, total, when it was placed, its due date and when Luma last read it. Values that need attention, such as a failed payment, are highlighted. When the due date has passed, it’s labeled **Overdue since**. Below that:

- A summary of the order’s current state: how many units are still to fulfill, whether it’s overdue, which issues need you and where sources disagree. It’s based on the source updated most recently. When more than one source reports the order, it also names that source’s status and payment.
- **Order lines** shows each item with its unit price, current quantity, total, fulfilled and remaining units. Beneath the current quantity, Luma notes how many were originally ordered and how many were reduced when those differ. Shopify’s reduced quantity includes removed and refunded units; it does not tell you why they were reduced. The total is the current quantity times the unit price after discounts, on the same tax basis as that price. When the source doesn’t report the current quantity, the total is **Unknown**. If the order comes from more than one source, choose the source under **Lines and charges from**; the choice applies to both **Order lines** and **Delivery and charges**. A line that differs between sources is marked, for example **Confirmed difference**. Fulfilled quantities count shipped, delivered or completed shipments that the same source reports. When a source reports neither current nor reduced quantities, Luma says it can’t tell how much remains instead of guessing. The item name opens its product. A line marked **Custom item or deleted product** has no product in the source; Shopify doesn’t say which of the two it is. **Variant deleted at source** means the product still exists but the ordered variant doesn’t. Each line’s **…** menu plans a quantity change, compares the line across sources or matches it with another source’s line.
- **Fulfillments** lists the fulfillment work each source reports. Work mirrored in two systems is never added together. Choose a fulfillment to open it.
- **Returns and refunds** lists the returns and refunds imported for the order, and the order’s return cases. **Open return case** beside the title starts one. See [Returns](/docs/returns).
- **Delivery and charges** shows what the chosen source reports about the order’s money and delivery: the shop currency and the currency the customer paid in, the sales channel and tags, the delivery and billing address, amounts when the order was placed and now, and any shipping charges (including removed ones), discount codes and tax lines. Amounts are shown exactly as the source reports them; Luma doesn’t recalculate them. **Not reported by** means the source doesn’t share that detail, which is different from zero; **None listed by** means it shares the detail and lists nothing. Addresses appear when you can view customers and the store is approved to share them. Otherwise Luma says access is restricted or that the source doesn’t share addresses. Expand **More details from** for when Luma read the source, whether the order was edited or is a test order, and the source’s own fulfillment status.
- **Stock reservations** appears when Luma keeps stock for the order. See [Review native stock](/docs/inventory#review-native-stock).
- **History** has three tabs. **Luma activity** lists the decisions, actions and results Luma recorded for the order, its issues and its workflow runs. **Order journey** shows placed, paid, reserved, picked, shipped, delivered and returned milestones when there is evidence for them. Split shipments and partial returns retain their own references. Each milestone says whether it is a provider observation, a Luma action or an inference. **Occurrence time unknown** means the source did not report when it happened; the separate observation time says when Luma read it. Known shipment-to-delivery durations can be compared with this workspace’s median and p90 over 90 days. Expand **Evidence references** to inspect the records behind a milestone. **Source readings** lists each state Luma read from a source, newest first. It shows what the source reported at that time, not every change the source made.

A section with nothing to show is its title alone, with a badge: **None reported** or **None imported** means the sources report nothing for it; **No access** means you don’t have permission to view it, so there may be records you can’t see.

The side panel lists the order’s **Issues**, its **Workflow runs** and its **Sources**, with when each source last updated the order and when Luma last read it. Choose an issue or a run to open it. **Manage links** opens the order’s linked sources. **Open in Records** shows the records behind the order, including each source’s own ID for it.

In the bar at the top, **Review issue** opens the issue that needs you. **More order actions** (**…**) plans a quantity change, opens **Linked sources**, the **Luma order record** or a source’s live details, or opens the order in Records. Each opens beside the order. Choose **Orders** in the bar to return to the list with your filters.

## When sources disagree

If an order exists in more than one system, **Source comparison** lines up what each source reports. It starts with the facts that disagree, marked for example **Confirmed difference**; choose **Show all facts** to see every compared fact and each line’s quantities, shipment state and location per source. To compare one line, choose **Compare across sources** in its **…** menu. Order numbers and dates are shown but not compared, because each system assigns its own.

**Current state** names each difference, for example: Payment differs between sources: “Paid” in Shopify and “Payment pending” in JTL-Wawi. The **Authoritative source** column shows which connection your workspace trusts for lines, cancellation, fulfillment, location and tracking. Each fact can use a different source. **Unknown authority** or **Conflicting authority** means Luma cannot choose a trusted source. Review the choice in [Source authority settings](/docs/workspace-settings#source-authority). Payment and totals have no authority rule yet. Systems often update at different times, so compare when each source last updated the order before you decide which one is wrong. Correct the order in that system; Luma shows the change after its next read.

## Plan a change before you make it

A change plan shows what a change to an order still needs before every system shows it. When sources disagree about a line’s quantity, a cancellation or a location, Luma lists the change under **Order lines** with whether it is still safe; choose **Review plan** to open the plan beside the order. To plan a change yourself, choose **Plan a quantity change** in a line’s **…** menu, enter the new quantity and choose **Plan this change**. **Show detected changes** returns to the differences Luma found. The same plan appears on an issue about the order. Plans change nothing.

Each plan shows:

- **Who shows the change**: each system’s current value and whether it already shows the change. **Unknown** means the system doesn’t report the value or its last read is too old.
- **Shipped**: units already shipped, counted once. Luma uses the system your workspace trusts for fulfillment, or otherwise the largest count any system reports. The same shipment in two systems is never added up.
- Whether the change is still safe: **Safe to change**, **Not safe to change** or **Safety unknown**, with the reasons. A quantity can go down only before any system starts fulfilling the line and never below the units already shipped. An address can change only before work starts, a location only while its work is pending, and an order can be canceled only before any work starts. Luma says **Safety unknown** instead of safe when a read is more than five minutes old, a read was incomplete, lines can’t be matched, no trusted source is set, no system reports fulfillment, or you don’t have access to fulfillment.
- **Steps**, in order. Steps marked **Luma** run through Luma with a preview and approval. Steps marked **Person** name who does them, such as the warehouse, the carrier, finance or operations. When work may already be in progress, the plan asks the warehouse to confirm or hold it first.

Today Luma can change quantities in Shopify itself; every other system is updated by a person. When a Luma step is ready, choose **Review in** and the store name to open [Change quantities in Shopify](#change-quantities-in-shopify) with the planned quantity filled in. You still preview and approve it there. If the step says you need a permission, ask a workspace admin. Plans update when the order is read again, for example after the warehouse starts packing.

A detected quantity difference also reads as one sentence, led by the system that holds order-line authority: for example “Luma ERP sets this quantity to 1; Shopify still shows 2; JTL doesn’t report it.” When the difference persists, Luma opens an issue, and the workflow [Carry an order quantity change to every system](/docs/workflows#workflows-that-start-from-issues) can carry it for you.

## Manage an order in Luma

When native orders are available in your workspace, open a Shopify order, choose **Luma order record** in **More order actions** (**…**), then **Accept into Luma**. You need **Manage Luma orders** and **Manage imports**. Luma creates a record linked to the existing order. The list continues to show one order with Shopify and Luma ERP as sources.

Before editing, choose **Luma ERP** for order lifecycle and order lines under [Source authority](/docs/workspace-settings#source-authority). In **Luma order record**, opened from **More order actions**, change the status or current line quantities. Expand **Billing address** or **Shipping address** to edit an address, then choose **Save order**. If another person changed the record, refresh it before trying again.

Cancellation is final. Reducing a line quantity makes the total unknown because shipping, discounts and tax adjustments cannot be calculated from the line price alone. Address changes do not create a new source version in order history. Edits stay in Luma; they are not sent to Shopify. To change a Shopify order itself, see [Change quantities in Shopify](#change-quantities-in-shopify). You can compare the sources to see their differences. An accepted record’s source identity remains fixed, so it cannot be linked or unlinked through the source controls.

## See live Shopify details

For an order from Shopify, choose **Live** _store_ **details** in **More order actions** (**…**). The details read payments, the customer, fulfillment holds, returns, refunds and events directly from your store, with the connection’s permissions. Luma doesn’t store these details. Choose **Refresh** to read them again, or **Open in Shopify admin** to open the order in Shopify.

**Open customer in Luma →** opens the order’s customer in [Customers](/docs/customers). Customer details appear only after Shopify approves Luma’s access to customer data and your store grants it. If an order has no Shopify order ID, Luma can’t read live details for it.

## Change quantities in Shopify

To change how many units of a Shopify order’s unfulfilled lines the customer receives, open the order and choose **Change quantities…** in the live Shopify details. You need **Change Shopify order quantities**, and the store connection must allow order edits. A store connected before Luma asked for this access shows it as missing until someone with **Manage connections** reconnects it.

1. Enter the new quantity for each line you want to change. Leave other lines empty. Choose **Restock removed units** to return removed units to stock.
2. Choose **Preview change**. Shopify calculates the new subtotal, total and outstanding amount without changing the order. When the customer pays in another currency, both amounts are shown.
3. Someone with approval permission approves the exact change in the order or in [Needs you](/docs/needs-you). Your workspace’s approval rules decide whether that must be another person.
4. The requester chooses **Send to Shopify**. Luma reads the order again and sends the change only if nothing relevant changed and Shopify calculates the same amounts. Otherwise review a new change.

A line can’t change once any unit is fulfilled, its fulfillment has started, or it was sent to a fulfillment service. Cancelled and closed orders can’t change. If order lines come from this store under [Source authority](/docs/workspace-settings#source-authority), you can choose any quantity. If they come from another system, such as JTL-Wawi, you can only set the quantity that system shows, so Shopify matches it; Luma needs a complete read of that system from the last five minutes. Luma doesn’t notify the customer. While [observe-only](/docs/apps-and-connections#observe-only) applies, Luma records the change but sends nothing to Shopify, not even the calculation.

**Accepted by Shopify** doesn’t mean done: the change is **Verified** only after a fresh read shows the new quantities. If Shopify’s answer is lost, the status shows **Sent, outcome unknown**. Choose **Check Shopify**; Luma reads the order to find out and never sends the change twice. **Not applied** means Shopify shows the change was never made.

## Link a Shopify order with its JTL-Wawi order

When Shopify passes an order to JTL-Wawi, Luma imports both as separate orders. Link them so Luma shows one order, while each system keeps its own lines, shipments and evidence.

1. Open the order and choose **Manage links** in the side panel, or **Linked sources** in the **…** menu at the top. When Luma suggests a match, the side panel says so; choose **Review matches**.
2. Under **Possibly the same order**, Luma suggests orders whose JTL-Wawi external order number matches. Compare the external order number, placed date and total. If more than one order shares the reference, check each source record before you choose.
3. Choose **Link** beside the right order. If Luma didn’t suggest it, search by order number under **Find another order** and choose **Review link**.
4. Under **Why is this correct?**, say why both records are the same order, then choose **Link records**.

Luma shows its progress while it links the records. Afterward, both source versions appear on one order. Links to either order open the combined order, and earlier issues, activity and readings stay available. Luma links suggestions automatically only when your workspace has an [automatic linking](/docs/data-sync#automatic-linking) rule for orders, and linking doesn’t change the orders in Shopify or JTL-Wawi.

To separate them again, choose **Unlink** beside a source, give a reason and choose **Unlink source**. That source becomes a separate order again, with its own lines and shipments. Every link and unlink is recorded with who made it and why.

Linking and unlinking need **Manage imports**. Without it, you see the linked sources and suggestions but not **Link** or **Unlink**.

## Match order lines

After linking an order's sources, choose **Match with another source’s line** in the line’s
**…** menu, or open **Linked sources** and choose a line under **Order line to match**. Review the lines from the other sources of this order, then choose
**Link** beside the same item. Check the item, size or variant and quantity unit, explain
why the match is correct, and choose **Link records**.

Matching names or SKU text are clues, so Luma waits for your confirmation. Each source
keeps its quantities and shipment evidence. This match can make a quantity correction
available in the change plan; the correction still needs its own review and approval.
Choose **Unlink** to undo a line match. Matching lines requires **Manage imports**.

## Fix a held or delayed fulfillment

To release a Shopify hold, reopen fulfillment or move the work to another location, open the fulfillment from **Fulfillments** on the order. See [Recover a Shopify fulfillment](/docs/fulfillment#recover-a-shopify-fulfillment).

## When an order is unavailable

- **This order isn’t in this workspace, or no connected system reports it anymore** means the link is wrong or every source removed the order.
- **You’re offline. This order may be out of date.** Reconnect to see current data.
- **You don’t have access to orders in this workspace.** Viewing orders needs **View orders**. Ask a workspace admin for access.

Shopify bundle components show the bundle sold with each line. Quantities, shipments and returns belong to those component lines; the bundle group is not an additional item. A changed catalog recipe does not alter an earlier sale. If a component was deleted, its SKU relationship stays unknown even when its SKU text is still visible.
