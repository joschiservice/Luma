---
title: Find your way around
description: Move around Luma with the sidebar, pinned pages, the command menu and keyboard shortcuts, get help for a page, and switch workspace or language.
---

Luma has the same layout on every page: the sidebar on the left, the page in the middle and tools in the top bar. This guide shows the quickest ways to reach a page, a record or an action.

## Use the sidebar

At the top of the sidebar is the name of your workspace, followed by **Search**. Below them:

- **Home**, **Needs you**, **Issues**, **Saved views**, **Activity** and **Insights**. **Needs you** shows how many items are waiting for you.
- **Pinned**: the pages you pinned.
- **Operations**: Inbox and Workflows.
- **Records**: expand this section for Orders, Customers, Products, Returns, Inventory, Fulfillment and Procurement. **All records** searches across record types. It expands automatically on record pages.
- **Installed apps**: pages added by installed apps.
- **Manage apps**, **Data sync** and **Settings**, with your account menu at the bottom.

Areas your role doesn’t include are hidden from the sidebar and from search. If you open one from a link, Luma says which permission is missing. See [Roles and permissions](/docs/roles-and-permissions).

To give your work more room, select **Collapse sidebar** or press **[**. The sidebar then shows icons only. On a narrow screen, select **Menu** to open the navigation.

## Pin a page

Select **Pin this page to the sidebar** in the top bar. The pin keeps the page’s filters, so you can pin a view such as issues from one system. You can pin up to 12 pages. Pins are personal and belong to one workspace.

To remove a pin, open the page and select **Unpin this page**. To rename, reorder or remove pins, open **Settings → Home & sidebar**.

## Search and run commands

Press ⌘ K on a Mac or Ctrl K on Windows and Linux, or select **Search** in the sidebar. Type a few letters to find:

- **Go to**: pages, with their shortcut letter.
- **Records**: orders, fulfillments, stock positions, SKUs, products and issues, from 2 characters. If your role includes **View procurement**, suppliers and purchase orders appear too.
- **Actions**: **Ask Luma**, **Use light theme**, **Use dark theme**, **Match system theme**, **Switch to Deutsch**, **Create workspace** and **Open documentation**.

Type a question of 3 characters or more and choose **Ask Luma** to ask it directly. Use the arrow keys to move through the results and Enter to open one. If you see **No matches**, try a page name, an order number or a SKU.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press **G**, then a letter within about a second, to open a page:

| Keys             | Page        | Keys             | Page        |
| ---------------- | ----------- | ---------------- | ----------- |
| **G** then **H** | Home        | **G** then **K** | Products    |
| **G** then **N** | Needs you   | **G** then **T** | Returns     |
| **G** then **I** | Issues      | **G** then **Y** | Inventory   |
| **G** then **R** | All records | **G** then **F** | Fulfillment |
| **G** then **A** | Activity    | **G** then **W** | Workflows   |
| **G** then **M** | Insights    | **G** then **P** | Procurement |
| **G** then **O** | Orders      | **G** then **C** | Manage apps |
| **G** then **U** | Customers   | **G** then **D** | Data sync   |
| **G** then **V** | Saved views | **G** then **S** | Settings    |

Other shortcuts:

- ⌘ K or Ctrl K opens and closes the command menu.
- ⌘ J or Ctrl J opens and closes [Ask Luma](/docs/ask-luma), even while you’re typing.
- **[** collapses or expands the sidebar.
- **J** and **K** move to the next and previous decision in [Needs you](/docs/needs-you).
- Esc closes the open issue or decision.

Single-key shortcuts don’t work while you’re typing in a field or while a dialog is open.

## Get help for this page

On pages with a guide, select **Help for this page**, the question mark in the top bar. The guide for that page opens beside your work, and reading it doesn’t change your filters or selection. Select **Open full documentation** to read every guide. You can also open the guides from **Documentation** in your account menu.

## Switch workspace

Select the workspace name at the top of the sidebar and choose another workspace, or select **Create workspace**. Each browser tab can show a different workspace. See [Sign in and choose a workspace](/docs/workspaces).

## Change the language

Luma is available in English and German. Change the language in any of these places:

- Your account menu at the bottom of the sidebar, under **Language**.
- **Settings → Language & appearance**.
- The command menu: **Switch to Deutsch**, or **Auf English umstellen** when Luma is in German.
- The language menu in the top corner of the sign-in page and other pages outside a workspace, and in the documentation.

Luma reloads the page to switch. The language applies to this browser. Menus, messages, dates and numbers follow it; records keep the text your systems provide. Names your team enters, such as custom roles, aren’t translated.
