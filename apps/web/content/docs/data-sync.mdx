---
title: Import history and keep data in sync
description: See what Luma imports from each connected system, how complete and current it is, and what to do when a sync needs you.
---

When you connect a store or ERP, Luma imports its history and then keeps it current. Syncing only reads data: Luma never changes records in your systems while it syncs. Open **Data sync** from the sidebar, or press **G** then **D**, to see what Luma holds from each connection.

## What Luma imports

Each connection has one sync per record type:

| Record type                                                                | Shopify       | JTL Cloud and JTL-Wawi |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- | ---------------------- |
| **Orders** with lines, totals and placed dates                             | Yes           | Yes                    |
| **Fulfillments**, such as fulfillment orders, shipments and delivery notes | Yes           | Yes                    |
| **Products** with their variants and SKU codes                             | Yes           | Yes                    |
| **Stock levels** per SKU and location                                      | Yes           | Yes                    |
| **Customers**                                                              | When approved | When chosen            |
| **Returns and refunds**                                                    | Yes           | No                     |

Shopify shares customers only when Shopify approved access to customer data and the store granted it. Contact details and addresses need their own approval. JTL shares customers only when **Import customers** was chosen when the connection was added. A record type the connection doesn’t cover shows **Not part of this connection**. One the system didn’t grant shows **The source didn’t grant access**. If a Shopify store shows **Not part of this connection** for returns and refunds, reconnect the store to add them. To change what a connection may read, open **Manage connection** next to it; see [Install apps and connect your systems](/docs/apps-and-connections).

Suppliers and purchase orders from JTL aren’t listed here. [Procurement](/docs/procurement) reads them for each purchase order.

## Follow the import

The top of the page sums up every sync, such as **Importing history**, **Up to date** or **2 syncs need attention**, along with **Records in Luma**, **Connections** and the **Oldest full check**. Below, each connection lists its record types with:

- **Status**, such as **Importing recent history**, **Reading changes** or **Up to date**.
- **Progress** while a read runs, as a count and a percentage when the source reports its total.
- **Coverage**: how many records Luma holds and the dates they cover.
- When it last synced, and when the next check or retry is due.

**Recent sync runs** lists each history import, change sync and full check, what started it, and how many records were imported, unchanged, waiting, not imported or no longer found.

You can use Luma while history imports; records appear as they arrive. Orders, Products, Fulfillment, Inventory, Returns and Customers show a one-line **Coverage** summary above their list. It says when the list is still incomplete or a source needs attention. Choose **Open Data sync** for the details.

## Understand coverage

Shopify imports recent orders first, followed by their fulfillments, returns and refunds, then older history in the background. **Recent history imported** means the recent period is complete; older history has its own progress and stays incomplete until it finishes. Fulfillments and returns show **Waiting for orders** until the orders they belong to are imported.

**Preparing history** means the source is building an export, so there may be no progress yet. You can leave the page. Luma continues on its own and picks up where it stopped after an interruption.

Coverage says exactly what Luma holds and never claims more. Some sources share only recent history: Shopify shares the last 60 days of orders unless access to older orders is approved. Data sync then shows **Only recent orders were shared** with the date the shared history starts. Luma keeps everything it imports, so coverage grows over time. When older records become available, Data sync shows **Older orders are now available** with a button to import them.

Some records may need attention. **Waiting for related records** means Luma imports them once the records they depend on arrive. **Couldn’t import** names records Luma couldn’t read, with the reason. **Not found in the last full check** lists records the source no longer returns. Luma keeps them, so check whether they were deleted in the source.

## How Luma stays current

- When a system sends change notifications, as Shopify does, most changes arrive within seconds. A busy system can delay them.
- Luma also checks for changes every few minutes. Sources that can’t list recent changes are reread in full every 30 minutes.
- Once a day, Luma rereads every record in a **full check**.

Luma recognizes records it already has. Importing the same history again, or receiving a change twice or out of order, never creates duplicates, and an older version never replaces a newer one. A record imported from history shows its state at import time; earlier changes at the source aren’t reconstructed.

## Run, pause or re-import a sync

If your role includes **Manage imports**, each record type has an actions menu:

- **Sync now** reads from the source again right away.
- **Check all records** starts a full check.
- **Re-import history** rereads everything the source shares. Records already in Luma stay available and aren’t duplicated. Large histories can take a while.
- **Pause** stops the sync. Records already imported stay available. **Resume** continues it.

Without **Manage imports**, you can follow every sync but not change it.

## Automatic linking

Luma keeps records from different sources separate until someone links them. If your store and your ERP use the same SKU codes, or JTL-Wawi records the Shopify order number on each order, a linking rule can link them for you.

If your role includes **Manage imports**:

1. In **Data sync**, go to **Automatic linking** and choose **Add linking rule**.
2. Under **Record type**, choose **SKUs: unique code** or **Orders: JTL reference to Shopify**.
3. Choose the two connections. For orders, choose the Shopify store whose order numbers JTL-Wawi records.
4. Choose **Preview matches**. Luma shows how many matches it can link and why it skips others. Open **First record** and **Other record** on an example to compare them.
5. Choose **Enable automatic linking**. The rule links the current matches and keeps linking new ones.

Luma links only clear matches. SKU codes match regardless of letter case and spaces at the start or end. Each code must belong to exactly one SKU in each connection, with the same unit. An order matches only when its external order reference in JTL-Wawi matches exactly one order number in the Shopify store. Similar dates, totals, names or email addresses are never enough. Luma skips ambiguous and conflicting matches and records someone unlinked by hand; link those yourself if you change your mind. Linked records keep their own quantities, prices and order states.

A rule shows **Waiting for data** until Luma has read both connections completely and successfully within the last day. Each rule shows how many links it applied and when Luma last checked. A rule runs with the access of the person who enabled it. If that person loses **Manage imports**, the rule shows **Needs access** until someone who has it chooses **Preview and enable**. **Disable** stops new links and keeps the existing ones. To separate a single record, choose **Unlink** under its **Linked sources**.

## When a sync needs you

- **Retrying**: a read failed and Luma tries again automatically, waiting a little longer each time.
- **Failing**: it failed several times in a row. The message explains why. Fix the cause, for example by reconnecting the connection, then choose **Retry now**.
- **Needs someone to resume**: the person the sync ran as no longer manages the workspace. Someone with **Manage imports** chooses **Resume with my access**, and the sync continues with their access.
- **Access removed**: the connection no longer grants this record type. Reconnect it with the access in [Apps](/docs/apps-and-connections#change-or-reconnect-a-connection).
- **Connection unavailable**: the connection isn’t active. Syncing pauses until it is; records already imported stay in Luma.
- **Paused**: someone paused it.

If a store or ERP stops accepting Luma, for example because its token was revoked or the Luma app was removed there, the connection shows **Access revoked** and an issue opens in **Issues**. Reconnect from the issue or the connection and enter new access. The connection keeps its records and links, and the issue closes after the next complete sync. Disconnecting a connection yourself opens no issue.

To link the same SKU across sources or correct Shopify stock from your ERP, see [Inventory](/docs/inventory).
