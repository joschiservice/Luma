---
title: Unblock held and delayed fulfillment
description: Find fulfillment work that is held or not moving, see why, recover Shopify fulfillments with approval and check that the work moved.
---

Fulfillment shows the work your connected systems report for each order: **fulfillment orders**, the work a location must pick and pack, and **shipments** that left a location. Each system’s work is listed separately, even when a store and an ERP hold the same order. Open **Fulfillment** in the sidebar or press **G**, then **F**.

## Find what needs attention

The page opens on **Needs attention**: held, delayed and failed fulfillments, longest wait first. A fulfillment needs attention when it shows one of these signals:

- **On hold**: the source stopped open work, for example because of an incorrect address or a payment that hasn’t arrived.
- **Past fulfill-by date**: open work passed the date the source set for fulfilling it.
- **No progress**: open work has reported no progress for 48 hours. Work scheduled to start later doesn’t count.
- **Delivery problem**: the carrier reports an attempted, delayed or failed delivery.
- **Late delivery**: a shipment passed its estimated delivery date and isn’t delivered.
- **Failed**: the source reports that the fulfillment failed.

The other built-in views are **On hold**, **Delayed**, **Open work**, **In transit** and **All**. The tabs above the list show how many fulfillments each view holds under your search and filters; the tabs also include the views you and your team saved. Opening **Fulfillment** from the sidebar returns to the view you used last, with any changes you hadn't saved.

Search by order number, tracking number, location or source ID. **Source**, **Status**, **Signals**, **Location** and **Shipping stage** above the list narrow it in one click. For anything else, choose **Filter** and pick a field, such as **Type**, **Holds**, **Carrier**, **Carrier status**, **Delivery type**, **Has tracking**, **Fulfill by**, **Estimated delivery** or **Order placed**, then a condition and a value. Holds a source doesn't report, such as JTL-Wawi's, are unknown: they match **is unknown**, never a hold reason or **is empty**. The sort menu names the current order, such as **Last progress**; choose it to sort by **Deadline**, **Order number**, **Location** or a date, or flip the direction. Work without the sorted value comes last either way. **Clear** removes every filter and the search.

An empty view, such as **No fulfillments need attention**, describes that view only. **No fulfillments match these filters** means the search or filters exclude everything. **No fulfillments yet** means no connected system has reported fulfillment work; check [Data sync](/docs/data-sync). When a view uses a filter you can't use, Luma says so instead of showing more work than the view means; choose **Remove unsupported conditions**.

## Arrange the list

Choose **Display** to change how the list looks:

- **Layout**: **Table** shows columns, **List** shows one row per fulfillment with the properties you choose, and **Board** shows a column per group, such as one per shipping stage. You can't move work between board columns, because the stage comes from your connected systems.
- **Columns** or **Properties**: choose what each fulfillment shows and in which order.
- **Group by**: group by status, signal, shipping stage, carrier status, type, source, location, carrier or delivery method, and dates by day, week or month. Work with two signals appears in both groups. **Incomplete** means a source is still importing its fulfillment history.
- **Open records in a preview**: choosing a fulfillment shows what blocks it, the next safe step and its items beside the list. From the preview, you can narrow the list to its status, type, source, location or carrier, or hide them.

## Save and share views

When you change filters, sorting, grouping or display, the view is marked **Modified**. Choose **Save as new view** to keep your changes, **Save** to update a saved view you can edit, or **Reset** to undo them. See [Saved views](/docs/saved-views). A saved view can also show metrics and charts above the list.

## Work from the keyboard

**J** and **K** move through the work, **Enter** opens it, **Space** opens it in the preview, **X** selects it, **/** goes to search, **F** adds a filter and **?** lists every shortcut.

## Read a fulfillment

Open a fulfillment to see:

- **Next safe step**: what’s happening and what to do about it, such as resolving the hold reason first or checking a shipment with the carrier.
- **Source facts**: type, status, holds, location, delivery method, deadlines, carrier status and tracking. A fact the source doesn’t report is marked as not reported rather than left out.
- **Progress evidence**: when the source last reported progress, the last change Luma saw and when Luma last read it, plus workflow runs for the order.
- **Items** in this fulfillment, compared with the quantity ordered.
- **Source timeline**: each state Luma read, newest first, with what changed since the read before.
- The **Order**, **Other fulfillments of this order**, **Issues** and **Luma activity**.

Customer, payment and address details stay on the order, which reads them live from the store.

## Recover a Shopify fulfillment

For an open Shopify fulfillment order, **Fulfillment recovery** appears under **Next safe step**. You can also open it from a fulfillment order in an order’s live Shopify details. It needs **Recover Shopify fulfillments**; without it, the panel doesn’t appear.

Fix the reason for a hold before you release it. Releasing an incorrect-address hold without correcting the address ships to the wrong place.

1. Choose **Refresh fulfillment** if the details might be old, so Luma reads the fulfillment from Shopify.
2. Under **Recovery action**, choose what to do:
   - **Release one hold**: choose the **Hold to release**. Luma releases only this hold; other holds stay in place.
   - **Reopen fulfillment**: open the fulfillment order for work now, for example one Shopify scheduled to start later.
   - **Move remaining items**: choose the **Destination**. All remaining items move; items already fulfilled stay at their original location.
3. Choose **Preview recovery**. The preview names the exact change and when it expires. If the order changes in Shopify before then, review it again.
4. Someone else whose role includes **Recover Shopify fulfillments** and **Approve or reject changes** opens the same fulfillment and chooses **Approve action** or **Reject**. You can’t approve your own recovery.
5. After approval, choose **Check approval** to update the review, then **Send to Shopify**. Only the person who requested the recovery can send it.

Recoveries waiting for approval, approved recoveries waiting to be sent and results waiting for a check also appear in [Needs you](/docs/needs-you).

Shopify decides which actions are possible. If an action isn’t available, the panel says why, for example that the fulfillment order is closed or canceled, that the selected hold no longer exists, or that Shopify can’t move all remaining items to the chosen location. **Reconnect Shopify and allow Luma to change fulfillments** means the store has to be reconnected by someone with **Manage connections**; see [Apps and connections](/docs/apps-and-connections#change-or-reconnect-a-connection).

Luma checks Shopify again just before it sends. If the fulfillment changed, the preview expired, the approval rules changed or the action was rejected, nothing is sent. Choose **Review another action** to start again. When a fulfillment has several recoveries, choose one under **Saved recoveries**.

## Check that the work moved

A recovery has three separate steps, and each shows its own state:

- **Review and approval**: the action is waiting for approval, or approved.
- **Shopify accepted**: Shopify accepted the request. The work hasn’t necessarily moved.
- **Progress verified**: a fresh read of Shopify shows fewer unfulfilled items.

Choose **Check fulfillment progress** to read Shopify again. Until progress is verified, treat the fulfillment as still stuck. Luma counts it as recovered only when a fresh read shows fewer units remaining.

Correcting an order’s shipping method is different from fulfilling it: it changes how the order will ship, not whether it shipped. **Progress evidence** shows whether Luma has since seen the order shipped or its work completed.

## When the outcome is unclear

**Outcome unclear** means Luma couldn’t tell whether Shopify received the action, for example because the connection was interrupted. Luma never sends the same action twice. Check the order in Shopify and choose **Check fulfillment progress** before you start another recovery.

## Other sources and delivery problems

For other systems, and for shipments that already left, **Next safe step** tells you where to act: in the source system, at the fulfilling location or with the carrier. JTL-Wawi doesn’t report holds, so its holds show as not reported. Luma shows the new state after its next read.

Viewing fulfillment needs **View fulfillment**. You can reach a fulfillment from an order, an issue about a fulfillment, **Records** and search.

## Review Starshipit shipping quantities

A linked Starshipit order shows its shipping and carrier evidence beside the order's other
sources. Unknown handoff means Luma has not confirmed who has the parcel. A printed label
alone does not establish dispatch. Check the read age and carrier update time before acting.
Split shipments keep their tracking separately; Luma does not guess how items were split.

Before a label is created, a quantity change plan can offer **Correct unshipped shipping
quantities**. Open the plan's review, check each old and proposed quantity, then choose
**Review correction**. The target must match your authoritative order source. If approval
is required, a permitted approver chooses **Approve correction** or **Reject correction**.
The requester then chooses **Send approved correction**.

Use **Check current result** until a fresh Starshipit read confirms the quantities. If the
outcome is uncertain, Luma reads again and never sends that same correction twice. When
writes are off, no correction is sent. Once labels exist or shipments have left, follow
the warehouse or carrier handoff in the plan.

## Publish a dispatched parcel to Shopify

When shipment publishing is available in your workspace, **Publish shipment to Shopify**
appears on Shopify fulfillment records and in the order's Shopify details. It needs
**Publish Shopify shipments**. This records an actual dispatch in Shopify; it creates no
carrier shipment or label.

1. Choose the dispatched parcel. A fresh carrier dispatch or delivery read is required;
   a printed label is not enough.
2. Choose **Create Shopify fulfillment**, the fulfillment order and warehouse, and the
   exact units that left in this parcel. For a split shipment, select only this parcel's
   items. If the carrier does not identify the parcel's items or warehouse, confirm the
   allocation against the warehouse dispatch record.
3. Choose whether to notify the customer. Notifications start off.
4. Choose **Save exact preview**. Someone else with approval permission and
   **Publish Shopify shipments** approves the exact allocation, tracking and notification
   choice. After approval, refresh the shipping evidence and choose
   **Publish approved shipment** as the requester.

To correct tracking already recorded in Shopify, choose **Update existing tracking** and
the exact Shopify shipment. Review and approve its tracking and notification choice in
the same way.

A successful send is not independent proof. **Check Shopify again** reads the exact
allocation and tracking. If the send result is unknown, Luma reads Shopify to reconcile it
and never resends the action. Large or incomplete source reads, changed warehouse work
and cancelled orders require review in Shopify.
