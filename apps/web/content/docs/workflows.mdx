A workflow reacts to something — a person starting it, an event, a condition such as low stock, or a detected issue — and runs a fixed list of steps. Every action it takes passes workspace policy and approval before it runs, and a separate check confirms the result.

## Read the library

The library lists published workflows and your drafts.

- **Active** workflows have an approved scope and run when their trigger happens.
- **Inactive** workflows are published but not switched on yet.
- **Prerequisites missing** means something must change first. The first reason is shown on the row; open the workflow for the full list.
- **Drafts** are workflows being written. Drafts never run.

## Open a workflow

The overview shows when it starts, what it acts on and what success means. **How it works** lists each step in order. **Recent runs** shows every run with its decisions, actions and result checks. An accepted action is not success on its own; only a passed result check confirms it.

## Start something new

Choose **New workflow** to start from a template or build your own. See [Create a workflow](/docs/workflows/create).
