---
title: Understand a customer
description: Find the right customer and inspect their orders, service history and Luma activity.
---

Open **Customers** or press **G**, then **U**. The list shows customers that your connected stores share with Luma. From an order’s Shopify details, choose **Open customer workspace** to go straight to that order’s customer.

## Find the right customer

Search by name, email address, phone number or the store’s customer ID. Filter by **Account** or **Source**, and sort by **Customer since**, **Orders** or **Name**. Each store keeps its own customers: the same person who bought in two stores appears twice, once per store. Compare the source and contact details before choosing one.

Search text is not saved in the page link, so links you share or pin never contain a customer’s name or email address. Filters and sorting are saved.

A customer appears after data sync imports them. **Coverage** above the list shows which stores have been read and when. If a link points to a customer Luma hasn’t imported yet, the page says so.

## Read a customer

The customer page shows the contact details and address the store shares, the order count and amount spent the store reports, the account state and tags. **Not shared** means the store withholds that field. **Not reported** means the store returned no value.

**Order history** is read live from the store, with payment, fulfillment and totals. Use **Next** and **Previous** to page through it. Shopify usually limits order history to 60 days unless older-order access is granted.

Select an order to inspect fulfillment, returns and refunds. When Luma has a confirmed mapping for that order, its open issues and recent Luma actions appear below. **Open in Luma** opens the full order, evidence and workflow history. A missing or ambiguous mapping is shown explicitly.

## When information is unavailable

A permission notice is different from an empty history. Ask someone whose role includes **Manage apps**, such as an Admin, to check the store connection and approved customer access in **Apps**. Order history, returns and fulfillment can have different permissions from customer profiles.

Choose **Refresh orders** to read the order history again, or **Refresh customer** to reload the profile.
