---
title: Understand a customer
description: Find the right customer and inspect their orders, service history and Luma activity.
---

Open **Customers** or press **G**, then **U**. Choose a source to browse its customers. From an order’s Shopify details, choose **Open customer workspace** to follow that order’s customer directly.

## Find the right identity

Search by name, email or order number when the source permits it. Name and email search require approved contact access. If several customers match, compare their source IDs and contact details before choosing one. Customer identities remain separate across stores.

Customer links identify the source and customer. Search text is not included in the link.

## Read customer history

The customer summary shows permitted contact fields, the source customer ID, order count and amount spent. **Access unavailable** means a field is withheld. **Not provided** means the source returned no value.

The order history lists orders visible to the connection, with payment, fulfillment and totals. Use **Next** and **Previous** to page through history. Shopify usually limits order history to 60 days unless older-order access is granted.

Select an order to inspect fulfillment, returns and refunds. When Luma has a confirmed mapping for that source order, its open issues and recent Luma actions appear below. **Open in Luma** opens the full order, evidence and workflow history. A missing or ambiguous mapping is shown explicitly.

## When information is unavailable

A permission notice is different from an empty history. Ask someone whose role includes **Manage apps**, such as an Admin, to check the source connection and approved customer access in **Apps**. Returns and fulfillment can have different permissions from customer and order records.

Choose **Refresh** to read the current customer again. Switching sources, workspaces or sessions clears the previous customer view.
