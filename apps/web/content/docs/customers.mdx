---
title: Find and understand a customer
description: Find a customer from a connected store or ERP, read their profile and see their orders with the issues and actions on them.
---

Customers shows the customer profiles your connected stores and JTL share with Luma. Use it to check who a customer is, what they ordered and what Luma did on their orders. Open **Customers** in the sidebar or press **G**, then **U**. From an order’s live Shopify details, **Open customer in Luma →** opens that order’s customer directly.

## Find a customer

Search by name, email address, phone number or the customer’s ID in the store. Narrow the list with **Account** and **Source**, and sort by **Customer since**, **Orders** or **Name**. **Account** shows whether the customer has a store account: **Has account**, **No account** (for example after a guest checkout), **Invited** or **Declined invite**.

Each store and ERP keeps its own customers, and Luma never combines customers from different sources by name or email address. Someone who bought in two stores, or who is in both Shopify and JTL, appears once for each source. Compare the source and contact details before you choose.

Your search text isn’t saved in the page address, so links you share or pin never contain a customer’s name or email address. Filters and sorting are saved.

**Coverage** above the list shows which stores have been imported and when. If a link points to a customer Luma hasn’t imported yet, the page says so; choose **Show all customers** to return to the list.

## Read a customer

The customer page shows the store and the customer’s ID in it, then:

- **Contact**: email, phone and address, as far as the store shares them.
- A section named after the store, such as **In Main Store**: the order count and amount spent the store reports, when the customer was created, their account state and tags. JTL reports no order count or amount spent, so those are left out for JTL customers.
- **Source and evidence**: the store, when it last updated the profile and when Luma last read it. Point to the store’s name to see the customer’s ID there.

**Not shared** means the store withholds that field from Luma. **Not reported** means the store sent no value. Choose **Refresh customer** to load the profile again.

## Follow the order history

**Order history** lists the orders Luma imported that the customer’s store or ERP attributes to them. Each order shows its status, payment, fulfillment, total and sources, and **Needs you** when it has an open issue. Sort by order number or placed date, and use the page controls to move through it.

An order that Luma links across sources, such as a Shopify order that JTL also imported, appears once and names both sources. Open an order to see its evidence, issues and workflow runs; **Back** returns to the customer.

The history includes only orders Luma has imported. Shopify shares orders from the last 60 days unless access to older orders is on for the store. Guest orders without a customer account appear in no customer’s history.

## When customer data is missing

- **Customers aren’t shared yet** means no connected store shares customers with Luma. Shopify shares customers only after Shopify approves Luma’s access to customer data and your store grants it.
- **Not shared** on contact details or the address means that part isn’t approved for sharing. The rest of the profile is still shown.
- A store connected before Luma could read customers must be reconnected to share them. The page names each store that needs it, including stores that haven’t granted access to customers.
- JTL shares customers only when **Import customers** was chosen when the connection was added. To add it to an existing JTL connection, add the connection again with that option. See [Apps and connections](/docs/apps-and-connections#choose-what-luma-may-change-in-jtl).
- **No orders yet** in the order history means Luma hasn’t imported an order for this customer. Orders already imported before the source shared customers are matched to them on the next full read.
- If the order history says you need access to orders, ask a workspace admin for **View orders**.
- **No longer in** a source, such as **No longer in JTL-Wawi**, means the source’s last full read didn’t include the customer, for example because they were deleted there. The profile and order history stay for 30 days. If the source still doesn’t have the customer after that, Luma removes the profile. If it reports the customer again, the label goes away.
- **Customer not found** means the customer isn’t in this workspace, the store asked Luma to erase their data, or Luma removed them after they were missing from their source for 30 days.

Someone whose role includes **Manage connections** can reconnect a store in **Apps**. See [Apps and connections](/docs/apps-and-connections#change-or-reconnect-a-connection).

## Who can see customers

Customer profiles include names, email addresses and postal addresses, so they need **View customers**. The built-in roles include it; a custom role might not. Without it, Customers is hidden from the sidebar and search, and a link to a customer says you don’t have access to customers in this workspace. Ask a workspace admin for access. See [Roles and permissions](/docs/roles-and-permissions).
