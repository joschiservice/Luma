title: Start your day on Home

**Home** is the first page of each workspace. It shows the decisions that need you, what Luma is working on without you and whether you can rely on the data behind it. Press **G** then **H** to open it from anywhere.

## What Home shows

Home is made of modules. **View all** at the top of a module opens the page with everything it summarizes. Until you customize Home, you see:

- **Needs you**: the most urgent approvals, manual steps and outcome checks from [Needs you](/docs/needs-you).
- **Luma is handling**: issues Luma is investigating, fixing or checking without you, and how many it verified or resolved without help.
- **Connected systems**: whether each system is sending recent data. See [Check your connections](#check-your-connections).
- **Recent activity**: the latest decisions, actions and checks from the last 30 days. See [Activity](/docs/activity).

You can add more:

- **Resolution outcomes**: verified resolutions and how many Luma resolved without help. See [Insights](/docs/insights) for details.
- **Operational pulse**, shown as **Where work is waiting**: live counts for issues, monitored stock, purchase orders and connections.
- **Order flow**, shown as **Orders that need work**: order volume and fulfillment timing for a period you choose in the module.
- **Catalog health**: SKUs whose store and ERP listings differ, are missing or aren’t linked.
- **Fulfillment health**: fulfillment that is on hold, delayed or failed, and shipments in transit.
- **Returns**: return requests to review, returns ready to refund or close, and failed refunds.
- **Saved views**: quick access to the live views your role can open. See [Saved views](/docs/saved-views).

Modules only include records your role lets you see.

## Choose the time period

Use the menu at the top right to choose **Last 24 hours** or **Last 7 days**. The period applies to the counts in **Luma is handling** and **Resolution outcomes**. Decisions waiting for you and connection health always describe the current state, including older issues that are still open.

## Customize Home

1. Select **Customize**.
2. Select **Add module** to add one of the available modules.
3. Drag modules, or use the arrows, to change their order. Use the width button to show a module at full or half width, and remove modules you don’t need.
4. Select **Done**.

Changes save automatically. Your Home is personal and belongs to this workspace; nobody else sees your layout. **Reset to default** brings back the standard modules. You can make the same changes in **Settings → Home & sidebar**.

If you changed Home in another tab or browser, Luma loads the latest version and tells you so. If you remove every module, select **Customize** to add some again.

## Check your connections

Each system in **Connected systems** shows one of these states:

- **Recent data**: Luma read every kind of data it’s allowed to read from this system in the last 24 hours.
- **No data yet**: at least one kind of data hasn’t arrived yet, so health is unknown.
- **Data out of date**: some data is older than 24 hours.
- **Degraded**: the system isn’t responding reliably.
- **Checking connection**: Luma is still checking the connection’s access.
- **Disconnected**: the connection was removed. It stays in the list as history but no longer counts toward health.

When something affects all of Home, a notice appears at the top: **Connect your first system.**, **Waiting for the first data.**, **Some data is out of date.**, **Check your connections.** or **No connected systems.** Select **Manage connections** to fix it in [Apps](/docs/apps-and-connections). Until then, treat the numbers on Home with care.

## Refresh and offline

Home refreshes every 30 seconds. Select **Refresh** to update it now; point to it to see when Home was last updated.

If you go offline, Home shows **Updates paused while offline** and hides its health and totals rather than showing old numbers. It refreshes automatically when you reconnect. If Home can’t load them, it shows **Operations overview unavailable**; select **Retry**. A module that can’t load says so on its own, and the rest of Home keeps working.
