---
title: Roles and permissions
description: Decide what each person can see and do, create custom roles, and control what installed apps may use.
---

Every person in a workspace has one role. A role is a set of permissions, such as **View orders**, **Manage procurement** or **Approve or reject changes**. Open **Settings → Roles** to see what each role allows and who has it.

## Built-in roles

Every workspace has three roles that can’t be changed:

- **Owner** can do everything, including making other people owners. Every workspace keeps at least one owner.
- **Admin** can do everything except grant or remove ownership.
- **Member** can see operations, records and workflows, take decisions and use Ask Luma, but can’t change data or settings.

Owner and Admin also have the permissions of every installed app, and they gain new permissions as Luma adds features. Custom roles only change when someone edits them, so new features stay off until you grant them.

### What Members can and can’t do

Members can see orders, fulfillment, inventory, products, customers, returns, insights, issues, workflows, procurement and activity, and they can use Ask Luma. **View customers** includes names, email addresses and postal addresses, including the addresses on orders.

In [Needs you](/docs/needs-you), Members can take a decision to show they’re looking after it, but they can’t approve or reject changes. They also can’t work on issues, propose or apply changes, edit workflows, purchase orders or suppliers, manage connections, imports or apps, or change settings, the team or roles. For any of this, create a custom role.

## Create a custom role

You need **Manage roles**, and you can only grant permissions your own role includes.

1. In **Settings → Roles**, select **Create role**.
2. Enter a name, such as _Buyer_, and a description that helps people choose the right role.
3. Under **Start from**, choose **No permissions** or an existing role, then select **Continue to permissions**.
4. Turn on the permissions the role needs. Search for a permission, use **Granted only** to check your choices, or use **Grant all** for a whole group.
5. Select **Review and create**, check the list, then select **Create role**.

App permissions aren’t copied when you start from another role; grant the ones the new role needs. To start from a built-in role, open it and select **Duplicate**.

## Assign a role

In **Settings → Team**, choose a role when you invite someone, or select **⋯** next to a person to change their role. You need **Manage team members**, and you can only assign roles whose permissions you hold. Only owners can make someone an owner. See [Team](/docs/workspace-settings#team).

A role change applies from the person’s next action in Luma. Work Luma does on their behalf, such as imports and workflow runs, checks their permissions again at each step and stops with an explanation if a permission is gone.

## Change or archive a role

Open the role, change its name, description or permissions, and select **Review changes**. The review lists what is granted and removed and how many people the change affects. Changes apply from each person’s next action.

The **People** tab lists who has the role. The **History** tab shows who changed the role, when, which permissions changed and who was given the role.

To retire a role, give everyone who has it another role, then select **Archive** and **Archive role**. Pending invitations for the role are revoked; invite those people again with another role. Archived roles can’t be assigned, and their history stays available.

## What people without access see

Areas a role doesn’t include are hidden from the sidebar and from search. If someone opens one from a link, Luma names the missing permission and offers **See what your role allows**. Home, Needs you, Insights and Ask Luma only include records the person may see. Settings sections they can’t change stay visible but read-only.

## Approvals

Approving or rejecting a change needs **Approve or reject changes**, and the approver must be someone other than the person who requested it. Some changes also need their own app permission. For example, approving a JTL purchase order also needs **Send purchase orders to JTL**, so a buyer role can approve purchase orders without being able to change shipping methods.

To require approval for every change, see [Approvals](/docs/workspace-settings#approvals).

## App permissions

Installing an app needs **Manage apps**. Luma lists what the app will be able to use, such as orders or customer details, and you can only grant access your role includes. When an app acts for a person, it can never do more than that person’s role allows.

Apps can add their own permissions:

- JTL: **Send purchase orders to JTL** and **Change JTL shipping methods**.
- Shopify: **Recover Shopify fulfillments**, **Correct Shopify inventory** and **Change Shopify order quantities**.

Owners and Admins have these automatically. To give them to a custom role, edit the role in **Settings → Roles**; they appear in a group for each app.

When an app update asks for more access, its page shows **Review new access**. Until someone selects **Approve new access**, the app keeps working with its earlier access. Uninstalling an app removes its permissions from every role, and reinstalling it doesn’t bring them back. See [Apps and connections](/docs/apps-and-connections).
