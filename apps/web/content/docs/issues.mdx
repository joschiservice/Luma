title: Find and prioritize issues
description: Use the inbox to see what needs your attention and follow work through to resolution.
---

Issues bring together a detected problem, the evidence behind it, and the next step. Start with **Needs review** to find work that needs a decision.

## Choose a view

- **Needs review** shows issues that currently require your attention.
- **All open** shows issues that are not resolved, including work already in progress.
- **History** shows resolved issues. Open an issue to see how it was closed.

The summary above the inbox gives you a wider view of operations. Its totals are separate from the filters applied to your list.

## Narrow the list

Filter by issue type, severity, or state. Filters work together: an issue must match all selected filters to appear. Clear a filter or switch views if you cannot find an issue.

Use the pagination controls to move through results. **Refresh** checks for changes and returns a paginated list to its first page.

## Open an issue

Select a row to read the investigation beside the inbox. On a small screen, the selected issue takes the place of the list. **Open full page** gives you more room to read and a link you can share with someone who has access to the workspace.

Your filters stay in the URL. Returning to the inbox keeps that view. If an issue changes state while you are reading, it can leave the current view; its details remain open so you can finish.

Continue with [Investigate an issue](/docs/issues/investigate) to understand the findings, evidence, and recommended next step.

## Understand an empty inbox

**Nothing needs review** means no issue currently needs your attention. Check **All open** for ongoing work.

**No matching issues** means the current filters found no results. Adjust or clear them.

**No data received yet** or **No active connections** means monitoring does not yet have the coverage it needs. Check your connections before treating an empty inbox as confirmation that everything is healthy.

## Get help as you work

Choose **Help** in the top bar to open the guide for your current page. Search across the guides or follow article links without leaving your issue. **Open full documentation** opens the current article in a new tab.
