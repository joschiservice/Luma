title: Read operational trends and verified outcomes
description: See where work is waiting, how orders, fulfillment, stock, issues and purchase orders develop, which issues were verified as fixed, and open the records behind any number.
---

**Insights** shows your operations in numbers. It answers three questions, one per view: what is waiting right now, how orders and issues develop over time, and which issues Luma verified as fixed with or without a person’s help. Every number and bar opens the exact records it counts. Open it from the sidebar, or press **G** then **M**. To open a view directly, search for **Now**, **Trends** or **Outcomes** with **⌘K** or **Ctrl K**. You need **View insights**.

## Choose a view

Switch between **Now**, **Trends** and **Outcomes** at the top of the page. The toolbar next to them holds only the filters the view uses, so a filter is never shown where it has no effect.

The right end of the toolbar applies to every view:

- **Connections current**, or how many connections need attention. Select it to open Data sync and see which reads are stale or failing. Until you connect a source, it says **No connections yet**.
- **Live updates** says that the numbers in the view update as data changes. When updates stop, or the view’s numbers can’t be read, it says **Reconnecting…**, **Offline** or **Updates unavailable**, and sections hide numbers they can’t keep current.
- **Refresh insights** reads the view’s numbers and charts again. On **Outcomes**, it also moves the end of a rolling period, such as the last 30 days, to now.

The view and its filters are part of the address, so you can share a view, pin it to the sidebar or come back to it later.

## See where work is waiting

**Now** starts with **Needs attention**: the counts that need someone right now, such as issues needing attention, stock below its minimum and orders needing you. Select one to see its records. When every count is known and none needs anyone, it says **Nothing needs attention right now**. If some counts are unknown, it says so instead of reporting nothing.

Below is **Where work is waiting**: what may need your next step, grouped into **Orders**, **Issues**, **Monitored stock** and **Procurement**. A number that means someone has to act, such as issues needing attention or orders needing you, is highlighted when it isn’t zero. Select a number to see how it’s counted, a breakdown by reason and the matching records. The first page stays current. On later pages the list stays in place while you read; if the count changes, choose **Show latest** to return to the current first page.

Orders count once, even when they appear in several connected systems. The **Orders** group says when its data is out of date. Counts include only orders Luma has read, so a zero doesn’t prove there are none.

Below, **Backlog and risk** charts open fulfillment, monitored stock and purchase-order stages as they stand now. Use **Source** to show one connection’s fulfillment and stock.

## Follow trends

**Trends** charts orders placed and issues detected over a time range:

- **Time range**: **7 days** or **30 days** by day, or **13 weeks** by week. Days and weeks follow your workspace time zone, and weeks start on Monday. The current day or week is marked **Today so far** or **This week so far**.
- **Source**: **All sources**, or one connection. Issues are Luma’s own records, so they aren’t split by source.

Each chart starts with its total, the **Daily average** or **Weekly average**, and the **Busiest day** or **Busiest week**. The average uses only periods with complete data and leaves out the current one, so a partly imported or unfinished period doesn’t pull it down. Select the busiest period to open its records.

**Previous period** compares the total with the same stretch of the period before: for the last 7 days, the 7 days before them, up to the same time of day. For example, **+12% vs 140** means 12% more than the 140 counted then. Select it to open those 140 records. When either period isn’t fully imported, a source isn’t current, or Luma hadn’t detected any issues yet, it says **Not comparable** rather than showing a change that would only reflect missing records.

## What each chart counts

- **Orders placed**: orders by the day their source says they were placed, colored by their status today. An order linked across Shopify and JTL counts once. Orders without a placed time are listed below the chart instead.
- **Fulfillment backlog**: open fulfillment work by how long ago its source last reported progress. Each item counts under its first signal: on hold, past its fulfill-by date, then no progress for 48 hours. Work reported by two systems counts once in each.
- **Stock risk**: stock positions with an active replenishment rule, compared with the rule’s minimum. Stock counts only if it was read from its source in the last 5 minutes; anything else is **Stock unknown**. A recorded purchase order doesn’t mean the supplier confirmed it or that enough stock is on its way.
- **Issues detected**: issues by kind and the day Luma first detected them. A reopened issue stays on the day it was first detected.
- **Purchase orders**: purchase orders that aren’t closed or canceled, by the stage their evidence supports. **Received** means a goods receipt was booked in your purchasing system. **Overdue** means a supplier confirmation or delivery is late.

Fulfillment backlog, stock risk and purchase orders show the state right now. Luma doesn’t rebuild past states it never read, so these charts have no history and appear under **Now**. Open **About this data** on any chart to see its period, sources and limits.

## Tell unknown from zero

A zero means Luma checked and found none. Unknown means Luma lacks the evidence to count. Each chart says which one applies:

- The line under each chart names its sources and its **Data through** time: the oldest complete read across its sources. Later changes may not be counted yet.
- When a source limits the counts, it’s listed under the chart with its state, such as **Limited history**, **Importing**, **Stale** or **Not fully imported**. A source that isn’t fully imported may be missing records, so they can’t be ruled out. **About this data** lists every source, including complete ones.
- A hatched bar marked **Not imported** lies before a source’s imported history. Its count is unknown, not zero.
- When nothing can report the records, such as **No source reports orders yet** or **No active replenishment rules**, the chart shows no numbers rather than zeros. It offers **Connect a source** or **Set up replenishment rules**.

If a chart can’t load, it says **Chart unavailable** and hides earlier values, so they aren’t mistaken for current ones.

## Open the records behind a bar

Select a bar, a segment, a legend entry, the busiest period or a value in the table view. A panel lists the exact records counted there, as of the moment the chart was loaded, and says whether its total matches the chart. If records changed since, choose **Refresh chart** to count them again.

From the panel you can open each order, fulfillment, stock position, issue or purchase order, choose **Decide in Needs you** when a decision on the record is waiting, or open the matching view in Orders, Fulfillment, Issues or Procurement. The browser’s back button returns you to the chart with the same panel open.

## Use the charts with a keyboard or screen reader

Move between **Now**, **Trends** and **Outcomes** with the arrow keys and press Enter to open one. Each chart is one stop in the tab order. Use the arrow keys to move between bars, Home and End to jump to the first and last, and Enter to open a bar’s records. Each bar is announced with its period, total and parts. Switch any chart to **Table** for the same values as a table, with each value opening its records.

## Verified outcomes and human help

**Outcomes** shows which issues were verified as fixed, where people helped, and where Luma can’t tell yet. Choose the cohort in the toolbar:

1. Choose the **Environment**: **Live operations**, **Partner pilot**, **Simulated**, **Shadow mode** or **Unknown environment**. Anything other than live operations is marked **Excluded from live results**.
2. Choose the **Detection period**: **Detected in the last 24 hours** or **Detected in the last 7 days**.
3. Select a number, such as **Verified outcomes**, **Resolved without help** or **Human involvement**, to list its issues.

Each issue in the list shows when it was first detected and verified, whether it was eligible for automation, and a help badge: **No human help**, **A person helped** or **Help unknown**. Open **Evidence and measurement history** to see what the counts are based on, then open the issue for its source data and what Luma did.

How issues are counted:

- An issue counts once, however many attempts or retries it took. If the problem comes back, Luma counts a new, linked issue under **Recurring issues**.
- An issue stays in the period in which it was first detected, even when it’s verified later.
- **Verified** needs a separate, current check that confirmed the outcome. A change the connected system accepted, or an issue someone closed manually, doesn’t count as verified.
- Approvals, manual repairs and corrections by a person count as help. When Luma doesn’t have a complete record of who helped, the issue counts as **Help unknown**, not as no help.

An issue was resolved automatically when it was eligible for automation, verified, and has a complete record showing no person helped. Those issues count as **Resolved without help**. Home’s **Resolution outcomes** module shows the same live results as **Resolved automatically** and an **Automatic resolution rate**. [Workflow tests](/docs/shadow-mode) never count as live outcomes.

Where your workspace records purchasing results, **Outcomes** also shows **Observed purchasing outcomes**: whether stock stayed available after a purchase order was received, and where a stockout was observed or the outcome is unknown.

## Review resolution performance

When available in your workspace, **Outcomes** shows **Resolution performance** instead. In
the toolbar, choose seven days, 30 days or a custom interval. Custom bounds are explicit UTC
instants; the report shows them in your workspace timezone. Filter by connection, issue class
and environment. Live operations are the default; simulated and pilot work remain separate.

The report starts with the **Automatic resolution rate**, the **Change in detected issues**
compared with the previous interval of the same length, and how many issues are **Verified in
the sample**.

**Detected issues** counts first detection during the interval. **Verified in the period**
counts independent verification during it, including problems detected earlier. Eligible
work and human involvement still use the detection cohort, including pending eligible
work. An accepted write, failed check or manual closure never counts as verified.

Open any number to inspect its exact issues and retained evidence. Daily counts use UTC
days; select a day in the chart or its **Table**, or a recurring class, to narrow the
report. Recorded connection and workflow-version rows offer the same exact filters. Use the browser's Back button to
restore the prior interval and cohort. If the cohort changes while paging, refresh it.

The report labels small duration samples and incomplete attribution. An autonomous rate
appears only for a nonzero, fully classified eligible cohort. Comparisons use equal-length
periods with sufficient recorded samples and do not explain causes. **Data-through is
unknown** means the numbers cover recorded incidents, not complete provider coverage.
Workflow-version rows can overlap across attempts; do not add them to get total incidents.
