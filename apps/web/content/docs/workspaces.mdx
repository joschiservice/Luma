---
title: Sign in and choose a workspace
description: Create your Luma account, sign in with a password or passkey, open or create a workspace, accept an invitation and sign out.
---

Your Luma account is personal. A workspace holds one business’s connected systems, issues and workflows, and you can belong to several. You sign in once and then choose the workspace you want to work in.

## Create your account

1. On the sign-in page, next to **New to Luma?**, select **Create account**.
2. Enter your name, your email address and a password of at least 12 characters.
3. Select **Create account**.

Next, create a workspace or accept an invitation to one. If Luma couldn’t confirm that your account was created, try signing in before you create another account.

## Sign in

Enter your email and password and select **Sign in**, or select **Sign in with a passkey** if you’ve added one. A passkey skips both the password and the code step. See [Secure your sign-in](/docs/account-security).

If your account uses two-factor authentication, Luma then asks you to **Confirm it’s you**:

1. Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app and select **Verify**.
2. If you don’t have your device, select **Use a backup code** and enter one of your backup codes. Each one works once.

After too many incorrect codes, wait 15 minutes and sign in again. If the sign-in attempt expires, select **Sign in again**.

## Choose a workspace

After you sign in, **Choose a workspace** lists every workspace you belong to. Open one to pick up where you left off.

To switch later, select the workspace name at the top of the sidebar and choose another one. Each browser tab can show a different workspace, so you can keep two open side by side.

A workspace marked **Access suspended** still lists you as a member, but you can’t open it. Your account and your other workspaces aren’t affected, and your role is kept. Ask a workspace owner or Luma support to restore your access.

## Create a workspace

1. On **Choose a workspace**, select **Create workspace**. You can also find **Create workspace** in the workspace switcher and the command menu.
2. Enter a workspace name, usually your business’s name.
3. Select **Create workspace**.

You become the workspace’s owner. Its data is stored in the European Union. If Luma couldn’t confirm that the workspace was created, select **Check workspace list** before you try again, so you don’t create it twice.

Then [connect your systems](/docs/apps-and-connections) and [invite your team](/docs/workspace-settings#team).

## Accept an invitation

An invitation arrives by email with a personal link.

1. Open the link. If you aren’t signed in yet, sign in or create your account in the same browser tab, and Luma brings you back to the invitation.
2. **Workspace invitation** shows the workspace, the role you’re invited with and the email address the invitation was sent to.
3. Select **Accept invitation**. The workspace opens with that role.

You must sign in with the email address the invitation was sent to. If you’re signed in with another account, select **Use a different account**.

Each link works once and expires after 7 days. If someone resent your invitation, only the newest link works. If the link has expired or was revoked, ask the person who invited you to send a new one.

## Sign out

Open your account menu at the bottom of the sidebar and select **Sign out**. This ends your session in this browser for every workspace. If Luma shows **Sign-out not confirmed**, your session may still be active; check your connection and select **Retry sign-out**.
