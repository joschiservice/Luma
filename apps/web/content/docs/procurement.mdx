**Procurement** shows your suppliers and purchase orders, where each order stands and who acts next. Open it from the sidebar, or press **G** then **P**. **View procurement** lets you see everything. Creating, sending and changing orders needs **Manage procurement**.

## How an order progresses

Every purchase order moves through four milestones, so a draft is never mistaken for goods on the way:

- **Drafted**: the order exists in Luma. Nothing has gone to the supplier.
- **Sent**: Luma placed the order in your purchasing system, such as JTL-Wawi, and a separate read found it there. Your purchasing system passes it on to the supplier.
- **Confirmed by supplier**: the supplier confirmed quantities and dates, read from your purchasing system or recorded from a supplier message.
- **Goods received**: units were booked as received in your purchasing system. Only that booking counts. A supplier saying goods were shipped, or closing the order in Luma, doesn’t.

The order’s stage sums this up, from **Draft, not sent** through **Waiting for approval**, **Sending**, **Sent, waiting on supplier**, **Confirmed by supplier** or **Confirmed with changes**, to **Partly received** and **Received**. **Sending failed**, **Closed** and **Canceled** end or interrupt the path.

## Find an order or supplier

Switch between **Purchase orders** and **Suppliers** at the top. For orders, the tiles count those that **Need a response**, are **Waiting on supplier** or are **Drafts not sent** across the workspace; choose one to open that built-in view, and choose it again to return to all orders. Each row shows the stage, the next expected delivery and **Who’s next**: the buyer, an approver, the supplier, the warehouse, Luma or no one.

Search orders by reference, supplier or SKU. **Supplier**, **Status** and **Stage** above the list narrow it in one click. For anything else, choose **Filter** and pick a field, such as **Open items**, **Expected**, **Confirmation due**, **Next check**, **Who’s next**, **Managed in**, **Delivery location**, **SKU**, **Value** or a custom field, then a condition and a value. Use the sort menu or a column header to sort, for example by **Expected**, **Confirmation due**, **Value** or a custom field.

**Display** chooses columns, a table or a list, and grouping by supplier, stage, who’s next, status or where the order is managed. With a grouping you can also show orders as a board, one column per group. The board is read-only: Luma works out each stage from the order’s progress, so you can’t drag an order to another stage. Custom fields for purchase orders can be shown as columns too. You see only the custom fields your role can read; an order without a value shows **—** and matches **is empty**. A custom field that was retired shows as archived, and a view that filters or sorts by it, or by a field your role can’t read, shows a notice: choose **Remove unsupported conditions** to see the list.

For suppliers, filter by **Has open orders**, **Confirmation overdue** or a custom field, sort by name, date added, number of orders or a custom field, and choose the arrow beside a supplier’s order count to see its purchase orders.

In both lists, save a combination of filters, sorting and display as a view and share it with your team; saved views appear as tabs above the list. Opening **Procurement** from the sidebar returns to the view you used last. See [Views on list pages](/docs/saved-views#views-on-list-pages).

You can also find suppliers and purchase orders from search with ⌘ K or Ctrl K.

## Review supplier purchasing terms

On a supplier or Product record, **Purchasing terms** shows each supplier's article number,
price, pack size, minimum quantity, lead time and preference. Missing values show **Missing**,
so you can distinguish an unknown term from a zero minimum. Purchasing-system values show
when they were read and whether the read was incomplete.

Choose **Add terms**, or **Edit** beside a product, to record one value with a reason. Your
value appears next to the observed value it replaces; a later sync keeps both. **Sources
and price history** keeps previous prices and the person and reason behind each change.
A draft takes the current supplier article number and price when its currency is known.
Changing terms later does not change an existing draft.

## Create a draft

### Review a purchasing plan

When **Purchasing** is available, compare requirements by supplier. Each product shows stock,
its demand assumption, lead time, the order deadline and the quantity before and after pack
rounding. Expand **Inputs and sources** to check read times and active replenishment rules.
Missing or stale inputs withhold a suggestion. Existing drafts and orders link to ongoing work.

Choose the demand period to update recommendations. Select products for a supplier, choose
the receiving location and adjust quantities. The plan shows order value and the resulting
days of cover. Record a reason for a minimum-value exception, then request draft approval.
Each supplier has its own approval and verified draft. Sending remains a separate action.
If creation is uncertain, check the saved order; that check creates or sends nothing.

You can also record why you ignored a recommendation. **Insights** separates later observed
stock and receipt outcomes from results whose evidence remains unknown.

1. Choose **Add supplier** and enter the **Supplier name**. If the supplier exists in another system, add its ID as an external reference.
2. Choose **New purchase order**, enter an **Order reference**, pick the supplier and add lines with SKU, description, quantity and unit.
3. Choose **Create draft**. Nothing goes to the supplier until you send it.

**Mark as open** records that the order is in progress without sending it. **Close order** marks it complete in Luma and doesn’t cancel it in your purchasing system. **Cancel order** ends an unsent local order. Submitted purchasing-system orders cannot be cancelled here unless the purchasing system supports a verified cancellation. Each status change needs a reason, and closed or canceled orders keep their history. Orders marked **Managed outside Luma** belong to another system and can’t be changed here.

Replenishment Workflows can draft orders too. Such an order shows **Why it was ordered**: the stock that fell below the rule’s minimum, the proposed quantity, who approved it and whether Luma checked the draft.

## Link a supplier

To send orders from Luma, link the supplier to the same supplier in your purchasing system. On the supplier’s page, choose **Link to purchasing system**, or **Link supplier** on an order. Pick the **Purchasing system** and the **Supplier in that system**, set **Expect confirmation within (hours)**, and choose **Save link**. After that time, an unconfirmed order appears in [Needs you](/docs/needs-you).

The connection must allow sending purchase orders. If none of your connections can list suppliers, connect JTL-Wawi in Apps with **Allow sending approved purchase orders** turned on; see [Install apps and connect your systems](/docs/apps-and-connections#choose-what-luma-may-change-in-jtl).

Every item on the order must also exist as an item in the purchasing system. If something blocks sending, the order lists it under **Before sending**. The order and send review show recorded unit prices and estimated value when available. These estimates exclude tax. The purchasing system determines final supplier prices and tax.

## Send an order

1. On a draft or open order, choose **Send to supplier**.
2. Check **What will be sent** and pick a **Requested delivery date**.
3. Choose **Request approval**.

Sending is a commitment to the supplier, so someone with approval permission must approve it under your [workspace rules](/docs/workspace-settings#approvals). They see the request in Needs you and on the order, with **Approve and send** and **Reject**. The request expires after 24 hours; the order shows when. After approval, Luma places the order in your purchasing system once and then checks that it arrived. If sending hasn’t started, the requester can choose **Send now**.

To approve, a person needs **Manage procurement**, **Approve or reject changes** and the JTL app permission **Send purchase orders to JTL**, and can’t be the person who requested it.

## Check that it arrived

- **Sending**: Luma is placing the order. If your purchasing system doesn’t confirm the request, Luma checks whether the order was created and never sends it a second time. Choose the **Check** button with your connection’s name to read your purchasing system now.
- **Sent, waiting on supplier**: a separate read found the order in your purchasing system.
- **Sending failed**: your purchasing system didn’t create the order. The message says why. Fix the cause, then send again.
- If sending was rejected or the approval request expired, nothing was sent. You can send it again.

The order’s **Purchasing system checks** list each read Luma made.

## Follow the supplier

Luma reads the order in your purchasing system regularly and records each confirmation and goods receipt. Choose **Check for updates** to read it now. **Lines and stock** shows for each line what was ordered, confirmed and received, what’s still open, the requested and promised dates, and the current stock for the item.

When a supplier confirms fewer units or a later date, the order needs a response. Choose **Accept or follow up**, then:

- **Accept the change**: Luma expects the changed quantity or date from now on.
- **Follow up with the supplier**: note what you did, such as emailing the supplier, and when Luma should **Check again on**. If nothing changes by then, the order returns to Needs you.

For a missed confirmation or an overdue delivery, choose **Record follow-up** the same way. These come back until they’re resolved.

## Record supplier messages

Choose **Add message** and paste the supplier’s email, portal note or phone notes. Adding the same email twice keeps one copy.

If your workspace reads messages automatically, choose **Add and read**. Luma lists each commitment the supplier states, such as confirmed quantities, delivery dates, shipments and conditions, and highlights the words it came from. Changes, conditions and unclear wording wait for your review: **Confirm**, **Confirm and accept** or **Reject** them on the order or in Needs you.

You can always record commitments yourself: select the words that state them and choose **Record what this says**. To fix a recorded commitment, choose **Correct** and give a reason. Corrections are added to the **Commitment history**; nothing is overwritten.

Luma removes message text after a retention period; **Message details** shows the date. People with **Manage procurement** can choose **Remove message text now**. Recorded commitments stay.

## Supplier pages

A supplier’s page shows the counts **Open orders**, **Waiting on supplier**, **Need a response** and **Overdue**, every purchase order with its stage, recent messages and related issues. **Items and stock** lists what you buy from the supplier with **Available now** and **On order**. On order counts draft and open purchase orders; it isn’t confirmed incoming stock.

## Replenishment

The **Replenishment** panel on Procurement shows how many stock positions your replenishment rules watch and what to fix next. **Manage stock rules** opens the replenishment Workflow; see [Workflows](/docs/workflows).

## Email orders and supplier follow-ups

For a supplier without a purchasing system, add its email address and language on the
supplier record. On the order, choose **Draft order email**, select the Operations Inbox,
and review your company address and requested delivery date. Open the draft in the Inbox
to review its text and PDF, then request approval. Luma sends after approval. Verified email
acceptance does not mean the supplier confirmed the order.

For an overdue confirmation or delivery, **Draft overdue follow-up** uses the promised and
outstanding quantities. Its next check time moves only after the send is independently
verified. Replies remain on the same thread and order; changed quantities or dates need
review. Manual follow-up stays available without a connected Inbox. If an email bounces,
check the address and review a new submission.

## Record received goods and book stock

For an order sent without a purchasing system, record received quantities per line, when they arrived, their condition and the delivery evidence. Quantities are cumulative: if another delivery arrives, enter the new received total. Record damaged units separately. The order keeps earlier values and your reason. Orders whose purchasing system owns receiving must be received there.

A manual receipt records what arrived. Review its stock booking to make undamaged units available in the system that owns the receiving location. Shopify bookings show the stock before and after; another authorised person approves, and Luma checks Shopify independently after the write. If stock moved while awaiting approval, review a fresh proposal. If the outcome is uncertain, check the existing booking; Luma does not send it again.

Locations owned by Luma use the native stock ledger. Another authorised member reviews the exact postings. Good units enter available stock and damaged units stay in the damaged bucket. Check stock independently after posting. Later stock movements may leave the original exact quantity check inconclusive; the receipt and journal entry remain visible.

## Check incoming stock

Inventory and Product records show outstanding supplier arrivals and transfers separately, including their destination, quantity, evidence time and arrival confidence. A supplier-confirmed date is distinct from a requested or estimated date. Past dates are marked overdue. Stale evidence stays labelled. At locations owned by Luma, the native incoming ledger balance supplies the quantity. Incoming units do not become available stock until they are received and booked.

## Change a submitted order

When your connection supports revisions, open the order and choose **Request an order change**.
Review the existing and proposed lines, choose one quantity or delivery-date change, add a
product, or remove a position, and give a reason. An approver reviews that exact change.
Luma then reads the purchasing system again and keeps both order revisions in history after
verification. A received position cannot be changed, and an order must retain at least one
position. Make multiple position changes as separate revisions.

If an attempt is uncertain, choose **Check the purchasing system** to reconcile it. The
original change is not sent again. Supplier commitments remain visible alongside the
revision, so reducing the ordered quantity never erases what the supplier promised.

JTL-Wawi permits position revisions while an order is Open or Released. Its API does not
support purchase-order cancellation. Arrange cancellation in your purchasing system; marking
an order complete is not a cancellation. Orders with received goods cannot be cancelled.

The **Purchasing timeline** keeps plans, approvals, sent orders, supplier messages, receipts,
your decisions and stock verification together. Expand an event's evidence to see its
references. **Recorded in Luma**, **Provider observation** and **Independently verified**
mean different things: a supplier message or recorded receipt does not by itself prove
available stock changed. Stock booking history requires permission to view inventory.
